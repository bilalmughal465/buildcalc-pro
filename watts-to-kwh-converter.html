The Energy used figure is the total electricity used in kilowatt-hours for the hours entered, and the Cost row is the estimated price of that electricity at the rate entered.

How to calculate

Formula: kWh = watts ÷ 1,000 × hours. Cost = kWh × rate.

Worked example: 1,000 watts, 5 hours, rate $0.17 per kWh. Energy = 5.00 kWh, cost = $0.85.

Tips

1,000 watts running for 1 hour uses 1 kWh. That is the simplest way to check any appliance, before you add days or months.

Circuit Load Calculator

Check load in amps and as a percentage of circuit capacity from watts and voltage.




—

Formula

Current in amps = power in watts divided by voltage in volts. Load percentage = current divided by breaker rating, multiplied by 100.

How it works

Add up the watts of everything on the circuit and divide by voltage to get amps. Comparing amps with the breaker rating shows how close the circuit is to its limit. Continuous loads should stay well below the breaker rating, and any circuit change needs a qualified electrician.

FAQs

What if load exceeds 100%?
The breaker may trip and the circuit is overloaded. Move loads to another circuit or have an electrician assess it.

Is 80% a safe limit?
For continuous loads, staying at or below about 80 percent of rating is a common planning guideline.

Can I add loads myself?
No, electrical work should be done or verified by a qualified electrician.

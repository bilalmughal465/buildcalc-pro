Movie Watch Time Calculator

Multiply the number of films or episodes by their average length to plan a marathon without surprises.

—

Formula

Total time = count x average length.

How it works

For a marathon, add breaks between films — even 15 minutes each adds up over a long day and keeps the last film enjoyable.

Series vary more than films: episode lengths differ by show, so use the average for the specific series you are watching.

FAQs

Should I include credits?
Average runtimes usually include credits, so the total is already close. Add a little if you watch everything to the end.

How long is a typical movie?
Most modern films run 90–150 minutes, with blockbusters often closer to 150.

Can this plan a series binge?
Yes — enter the episode count and average episode length the same way.

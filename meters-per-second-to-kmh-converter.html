Convert m/s to km/h instantly with a simple, free calculator.

Formula

Km/h = M/s × 3.6. Multiply the value in m/s by 3.6 to get the value in km/h.

How it works

Enter a value in m/s. The calculator multiplies it by the conversion factor and shows the equivalent in km/h, rounded to at most four decimal places so long decimals stay readable. Negative values are converted the same way, since the factor applies in both directions of the scale.

FAQs

How many km/h are in 1 m/s?
One m/s equals 3.6 km/h. Multiply any value in m/s by 3.6 to get km/h.

Is this m/s to km/h conversion exact?
There are 3,600 seconds in an hour and 1,000 meters in a kilometer, so multiply by 3,600 and divide by 1,000 — a factor of exactly 3.6.

When would I need a m/s to km/h conversion?
Any time a measurement, label, or instruction is given in m/s and you need to work, compare, or record it in km/h — for example while shopping, travelling, cooking, studying, or planning a project.

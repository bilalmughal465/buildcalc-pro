How do calculators work?

Open a calculator, enter your own numbers, and read the instant result. Every calculator page shows its formula, and many show a worked example, so you can check the math yourself. Calculator pages also include frequently asked questions with direct answers.

What other tools are there?

Besides calculators, BuildCalc includes simple tools such as lists, counters, timers, planners, pickers and code generators. Some tools use free public services, for example live currency rates, live weather, QR codes and barcodes. Those tools say clearly on the page whether the result is live or entered by you.

Is BuildCalc free?

Yes. All calculators and tools are free to use, with no sign-up and no download. There is no paid tier. The Blog page adds free guides when they are ready.

Are results exact?

Results are estimates and general information only, based on the numbers you enter. For building work, money, health or legal decisions, always confirm with a qualified professional or your supplier before acting on a result.

Who is BuildCalc for?

BuildCalc is for anyone who needs a quick, checked calculation: homeowners planning a project, students doing math, small business owners checking prices and margins, cooks scaling a recipe, travellers planning a trip, and anyone who needs a simple everyday tool.

How BuildCalc is built, and what it is not

BuildCalc calculators are formula-based tools built by the BuildCalc team. Each calculator shows the formula it uses, so the result can be checked from the numbers entered. Results are estimates and general information only, not financial, legal, tax or medical advice, and not a substitute for a qualified professional. BuildCalc does not claim individual professional credentials for its calculators, and no calculator page should be read as professional advice. If a result looks wrong, please report the calculator name and the values entered, so the formula can be checked.

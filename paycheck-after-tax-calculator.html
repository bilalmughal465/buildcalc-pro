Paycheck After Tax Calculator

Estimate take-home pay per paycheck from salary and a tax rate you enter.

—

Formula

Take-home per paycheck equals salary times one minus tax rate divided by 100, divided by paychecks per year.

How it works

Real paychecks also lose pension, health, and other deductions, and tax is usually bracketed, so this single-rate estimate is rough. Enter the effective rate you expect for a closer figure.

FAQs

Why is the rate not built in?
Tax rates differ by country, region, and income.

What else reduces a paycheck?
Pension contributions, health premiums, and other withholdings.

Monthly vs biweekly?
Use 12 for monthly pay, 26 for biweekly, 52 for weekly.

Are these figures financial advice?
No — they are estimates for planning only, not financial advice. Check with a qualified adviser for decisions involving large sums.

What to write

To get the fastest useful reply, include: the calculator or tool name, the exact page link if you have it, the values you entered, the result you saw, and the result you expected. For a correction, include the formula or source you used, so the math can be checked.

Calculator requests

You can request a new calculator or tool, or a correction to an existing one. Requests are read, and useful, searched-for calculators are considered first. There is no promise that every request will be added, and no date is promised for a requested tool.

What not to send

Please do not send passwords, bank details, card details, government ID numbers or other sensitive personal information. No calculator or contact form on BuildCalc needs those details. If you sent such details by mistake, change them at once with the relevant provider.

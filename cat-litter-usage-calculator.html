Cat Litter Usage Calculator

Estimate monthly litter use and litter boxes from the number of cats.

—

Formula

Monthly litter equals cats multiplied by litter per cat per month; boxes equal cats plus one.

How it works

Each cat adds a similar amount of litter use, while the box rule adds one spare box to reduce competition and accidents. Litter type and scooping frequency shift real use.

FAQs

Why one extra box?
Cats may avoid a box another cat just used, so a spare helps.

Does clumping litter last longer?
Often yes, because only soiled litter is removed.

How often should I scoop?
Daily scooping keeps use lower and cats happier.

General information only, not veterinary advice.

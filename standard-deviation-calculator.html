Standard Deviation Calculator

Measure how spread out a list of numbers is around its mean, using the population formula.

—

Formula

Population standard deviation = square root of the average squared distance of each value from the mean.

How it works

Type numbers separated by commas. The calculator finds the mean, averages the squared differences from it, and takes the square root.

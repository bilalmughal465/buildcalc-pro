How to calculate

Formula: monthly bill = monthly kWh × rate + fixed fee.

Worked example: 900 kWh, rate $0.17 per kWh, fixed fee $10. Energy cost = $153.00, total bill = $163.00.

Tips

Add taxes and delivery charges from your bill if your utility charges them separately, because this estimate includes energy cost and fixed fee only.

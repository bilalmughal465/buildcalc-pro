Dog Treat Calories Calculator

Work out a daily treat calorie budget as a share of a dog's daily calories, and how many treats that allows.

—

Formula

Treat budget equals daily calories multiplied by the treat percentage; treat count equals budget divided by calories per treat.

How it works

The common 10% guide keeps treats from crowding out balanced food. Dividing the budget by calories per treat turns it into a countable daily number.

FAQs

Why 10%?
It is a common guide so treats stay a small part of the diet.

Do training treats count?
Yes. Every treat counts, including tiny training rewards.

What if treats are large?
Break them into smaller pieces to stay in budget.

General information only, not veterinary advice.

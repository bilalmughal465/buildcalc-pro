Markup Calculator

Set a selling price from cost and a desired markup percentage.

—

Formula

Selling price equals cost times one plus markup divided by 100.

How it works

Markup is added on top of cost, while margin is measured against price — a 50% markup gives a 33.3% margin, not 50%. Keep the two apart when pricing.

FAQs

Why is markup not the same as margin?
Markup divides by cost, margin divides by price, so the percentages differ.

Can I work backwards from a price?
Yes — margin on a price is profit divided by that price.

Should markup cover overheads?
Yes, price must cover both direct cost and overheads plus profit.

Are these figures financial advice?
No — they are estimates for planning only, not financial advice. Check with a qualified adviser for decisions involving large sums.

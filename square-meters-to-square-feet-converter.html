Square Meters to Square Feet Converter

Convert square meters to square feet instantly with a simple, free calculator.

Formula

Square feet = Square meters × 10.7639. Multiply the value in square meters by 10.7639 to get the value in square feet.

How it works

Enter a value in square meters. The calculator multiplies it by the conversion factor and shows the equivalent in square feet, rounded to at most four decimal places so long decimals stay readable. Negative values are converted the same way, since the factor applies in both directions of the scale.

FAQs

How many square feet are in 1 square meters?
One square meters equals 10.7639 square feet. Multiply any value in square meters by 10.7639 to get square feet.

Is this square meters to square feet conversion exact?
One square foot equals 0.092903 square meters, so the conversion factor is the square of the length conversion, not the length factor itself.

When would I need a square meters to square feet conversion?
Any time a measurement, label, or instruction is given in square meters and you need to work, compare, or record it in square feet — for example while shopping, travelling, cooking, studying, or planning a project.

Meters to Feet Converter

Convert meters to feet instantly with a simple, free calculator.

—

Formula

Feet = Meters × 3.28084. Multiply the value in meters by 3.28084 to get the value in feet.

How it works

Enter a value in meters. The calculator multiplies it by the conversion factor and shows the equivalent in feet, rounded to at most four decimal places so long decimals stay readable. Negative values are converted the same way, since the factor applies in both directions of the scale.

FAQs

How many feet are in 1 meters?
One meters equals 3.28084 feet. Multiply any value in meters by 3.28084 to get feet.

Is this meters to feet conversion exact?
This uses the internationally agreed definition: 1 foot equals exactly 0.3048 meters, so the result is exact before rounding.

When would I need a meters to feet conversion?
Any time a measurement, label, or instruction is given in meters and you need to work, compare, or record it in feet — for example while shopping, travelling, cooking, studying, or planning a project.

Bedtime From Wake Time Calculator

Work backwards from a wake time in whole 90-minute sleep cycles to find a bedtime.

—

Formula

Bedtime equals wake time minus cycles times 90 minutes, minus 15 minutes allowed to fall asleep.

How it works

Whole cycles are counted backwards from the wake time, with a small allowance for falling asleep. The result is a lights-out target, not a guarantee of waking refreshed.

FAQs

Should I count 15 minutes to fall asleep?
It is a common allowance; use more if you usually take longer.

What if the bedtime looks too early?
Try one fewer cycle, or an earlier wake time.

Does this fix insomnia?
No. It is a scheduling aid, not a treatment.

General information only, not medical advice.

Kilometers per Hour to Miles per Hour Converter

Convert km/h to mph instantly with a simple, free calculator.

Formula

Mph = Km/h × 0.621371. Multiply the value in km/h by 0.621371 to get the value in mph.

How it works

Enter a value in km/h. The calculator multiplies it by the conversion factor and shows the equivalent in mph, rounded to at most four decimal places so long decimals stay readable. Negative values are converted the same way, since the factor applies in both directions of the scale.

FAQs

How many mph are in 1 km/h?
One km/h equals 0.621371 mph. Multiply any value in km/h by 0.621371 to get mph.

Is this km/h to mph conversion exact?
One mile per hour equals 1.609344 kilometers per hour, so the factor is the same as for plain kilometers to miles.

When would I need a km/h to mph conversion?
Any time a measurement, label, or instruction is given in km/h and you need to work, compare, or record it in mph — for example while shopping, travelling, cooking, studying, or planning a project.

Working Days Between Dates Calculator

Count Monday-to-Friday working days between two dates, excluding weekends.

—

Formula

Working days = count of dates from start to end inclusive whose weekday is Monday to Friday. Saturdays and Sundays are skipped.

How it works

Pick the start and end dates. The calculator walks day by day and counts only Monday-to-Friday dates, inclusive of both ends. Public holidays are not excluded, because they vary by country and region.

FAQs

Are public holidays excluded?
No. Only weekends are excluded; holidays vary by place, so subtract any holidays in your period manually.

Is the count inclusive?
Yes. Both the start and end dates count if they are weekdays.

What if the range is a single weekend day?
The result is 0, since no weekday falls in the range.

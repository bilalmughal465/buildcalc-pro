Quadratic Equation Calculator

Find the roots of a quadratic equation of the form ax squared plus bx plus c equals zero.

—

Formula

Roots = (−b ± square root of (b squared − 4ac)) ÷ 2a. The value b squared − 4ac is the discriminant.

How it works

Enter a, b, and c with a not zero. The calculator checks the discriminant: positive gives two real roots, zero gives one repeated root, negative gives no real roots.

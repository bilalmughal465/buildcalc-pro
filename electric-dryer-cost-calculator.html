How to calculate

Formula: monthly kWh = watts ÷ 1,000 × hours per day × days per month. Cost = monthly kWh × electricity rate.

Worked example: 3000 watts, 1 hours per day, 30 days, rate $0.17 per kWh. Monthly use = 90 kWh, monthly cost = $15.30.

Tips

Watts are average running watts, not peak watts. Check your appliance label or bill, and use your actual electricity rate for the most useful result. Prices are editable estimates.

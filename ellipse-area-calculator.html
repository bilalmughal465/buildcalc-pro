Ellipse Area Calculator

Find the area of an ellipse from its two semi-axes.

—

Formula

Ellipse area = π × semi-axis a × semi-axis b.

How it works

Enter the two half-widths of the ellipse. The calculator multiplies them by pi. When both are equal, it becomes a circle.

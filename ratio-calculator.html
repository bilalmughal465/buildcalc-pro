Ratio Calculator

Simplify a ratio of two numbers to its smallest whole-number form.

—

Formula

Divide both parts of the ratio by their greatest common divisor to simplify it.

How it works

Enter two whole numbers. The calculator finds their greatest common divisor and divides both parts by it. For example, 24:36 simplifies to 2:3.

FAQs

What does the Ratio do?
It takes the values you enter and works out the ratio for you, showing the result straight away.

What should I enter?
Enter ordinary numbers in the boxes above. If a value does not make sense for this calculation, the page will ask you to check it instead of showing a wrong answer.

Can I use the result for homework or everyday maths?
Yes. It is a general-purpose maths helper; for exam or professional work, still check the steps against your own working.

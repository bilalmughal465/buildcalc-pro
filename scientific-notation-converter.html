Scientific Notation Calculator

Convert an ordinary number into scientific notation, a number between 1 and 10 times a power of ten.

—

Formula

Scientific notation writes a number as a mantissa between 1 and 10, multiplied by 10 raised to an exponent.

How it works

Enter any non-zero number. The calculator shifts the decimal point until one digit sits before it, and counts the shifts as the exponent.

FAQs

What does the Scientific Notation do?
It takes the values you enter and works out the scientific notation for you, showing the result straight away.

What should I enter?
Enter ordinary numbers in the boxes above. If a value does not make sense for this calculation, the page will ask you to check it instead of showing a wrong answer.

Can I use the result for homework or everyday maths?
Yes. It is a general-purpose maths helper; for exam or professional work, still check the steps against your own working.

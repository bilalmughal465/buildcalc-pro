How to calculate garden wall block

Garden wall blocks at about 1.125 blocks per square foot, with waste.

Formula: quantity = area × 1.125 × (1 + waste %) = 100 × 1.125 × 1.10 = 124 blocks.

Worked example

Example: 10 ft × 10 ft = 100 sq ft. Quantity: 124 blocks, about $372 at $3 per blocks.

Tips

Measure the full area, then subtract large openings if you want an exact count. Order a little extra for cuts and breakage, and confirm quantities with your supplier before you buy.

How much waste should I add?

Add 10% for straight areas and 15% for areas with many cuts, corners and openings.

Prices are editable estimates. The defaults are typical US figures, and your local price can be higher or lower.

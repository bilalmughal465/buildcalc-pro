How to Calculate Loan Payments

A simple guide with a worked example, a payments table and answers to the questions people actually ask.

Short answer

A $200,000.00 loan at 6% annual interest over 30 years costs $1,199.10 per month, $431,676.00 in total, and $231,676.00 in interest. Use the formula below with your own loan amount, rate and term, or try the Business Loan Payment Calculator for an instant result.

The formula

Monthly payment = P × r × (1 + r)n ÷ ((1 + r)n − 1), where P is the loan amount, r is the monthly interest rate (annual rate ÷ 12), and n is the number of monthly payments (years × 12). The formula gives principal and interest only. Taxes, insurance and fees are separate.

Worked example: $200,000.00 at 6% for 30 years

Step 1: the monthly rate is 6% ÷ 12 = 0.5% (0.005). Step 2: the number of payments is 30 × 12 = 360. Step 3: apply the formula: $200,000.00 × 0.005 × (1.005)360 ÷ ((1.005)360 − 1) = $1,199.10 per month. Step 4: the total paid is $1,199.10 × 360 = $431,676.00, so the interest is $431,676.00 − $200,000.00 = $231,676.00. That is the same result the Business Loan Payment Calculator gives for these numbers.

What changes the payment

The interest rate matters most, then the term, then the amount. A longer term lowers the monthly payment but raises the total interest: the same $200,000.00 loan at 6% costs $103,787.80 in interest over 15 years ($1,687.71 per month) but $231,676.00 over 30 years ($1,199.10 per month). A lower rate lowers both the payment and the total interest.

Common mistakes

The most common mistake is comparing only the monthly payment and ignoring the total interest. The next is forgetting that the formula covers principal and interest only, so taxes, insurance and fees come on top. The third is mixing up the annual rate and the monthly rate: always divide the annual rate by 12 before using the formula.

Frequently asked questions

What is the loan payment formula?

Monthly payment = P x r x (1 + r)^n / ((1 + r)^n - 1), where P is the loan amount, r is the monthly interest rate (annual rate / 12), and n is the number of monthly payments (years x 12).

What is the monthly payment on $200,000.00 at 6% for 30 years?

$1,199.10 per month. The total paid is $431,676.00, so the total interest is $231,676.00.

Does a longer term lower my payment?

Yes, the monthly payment falls, but the total interest rises. The same $200,000.00 loan at 6% costs $103,787.80 in interest over 15 years ($1,687.71 per month) but $231,676.00 over 30 years ($1,199.10 per month).

What happens if I pay extra each month?

Extra payments go toward the principal, which reduces the remaining balance, the total interest and the time to repay. The exact saving depends on your loan amount, rate and how much extra you pay.

Are taxes and insurance included in the payment?

No. This formula covers principal and interest only. Property taxes, insurance and any fees are separate and come on top of the calculated payment.

Decimal to Percent Calculator

Convert a decimal number into a percentage by multiplying by 100.

—

Formula

Percent = decimal × 100, with a percent sign added.

How it works

Enter a decimal such as 0.75. The calculator multiplies by 100, giving 75%.

FAQs

What does the Decimal to Percent do?
It takes the values you enter and works out the decimal to percent for you, showing the result straight away.

What should I enter?
Enter ordinary numbers in the boxes above. If a value does not make sense for this calculation, the page will ask you to check it instead of showing a wrong answer.

Can I use the result for homework or everyday maths?
Yes. It is a general-purpose maths helper; for exam or professional work, still check the steps against your own working.

Geometric Sequence Calculator

Find the nth term of a geometric sequence, where each term is multiplied by a common ratio.

—

Formula

Nth term = a1 × r raised to (n − 1), where r is the common ratio.

How it works

Enter the first term, the ratio, and n. The calculator multiplies the first term by the ratio raised to n minus one.

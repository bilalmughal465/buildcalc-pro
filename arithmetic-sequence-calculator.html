Arithmetic Sequence Calculator

Find the nth term and the sum of the first n terms of an arithmetic sequence.

—

Formula

Nth term = a1 + (n − 1) × d. Sum of first n terms = n ÷ 2 × (2 × a1 + (n − 1) × d).

How it works

Enter the first term, the constant difference, and n. The calculator gives both the nth term and the total of the first n terms.

Retirement Savings Estimate Calculator

Roughly project a retirement balance from current savings, monthly contributions, and return.

—

Formula

The balance is the growth of current savings plus the future value of monthly contributions at the monthly return rate.

How it works

This is a long-range estimate, not a retirement plan. Real returns vary, and withdrawals, taxes, and inflation are not included.

FAQs

Should I include inflation?
For a real-terms view, use a return net of inflation, for example 7% return minus 3% inflation is about 4%.

Does it say if I have enough?
No — compare the result with your expected retirement spending separately.

Why start early?
More years of compounding make a bigger difference than larger late contributions.

Are these figures financial advice?
No — they are estimates for planning only, not financial advice. Check with a qualified adviser for decisions involving large sums.

Estimates only, not financial advice. Figures are approximate and do not include all fees, taxes, or changing rates unless stated.

Waist-to-Hip Ratio Calculator

Divide waist by hip measurement for a simple fat-distribution indicator.

—

Formula

Waist-to-hip ratio equals waist circumference divided by hip circumference.

How it works

Measure waist at the navel and hip at the widest point, then divide. A higher ratio means more weight carried around the waist relative to the hips.

FAQs

What is a high ratio?
Rough guides are above 0.90 for men and 0.85 for women, but these are screening guides only.

Does it replace BMI?
No. It adds distribution information that BMI misses.

How should I measure?
Standing, relaxed, tape level and snug, not pulled tight.

General information only, not medical advice.

Refinance Savings Calculator

Compare an old mortgage payment with a new one to estimate monthly and total savings.

—

Formula

Each payment uses the amortising formula; monthly saving is old minus new, and break-even months are closing costs divided by monthly saving.

How it works

Refinancing can lower the monthly payment while extending the term, which may raise total interest — check both monthly saving and total cost, plus how long you will stay.

FAQs

What is break-even?
How many months of savings repay the closing costs.

Can a lower payment cost more overall?
Yes, if the new term is much longer.

Should fees be rolled into the loan?
That raises the balance — compare that option separately.

Are these figures financial advice?
No — they are estimates for planning only, not financial advice. Check with a qualified adviser for decisions involving large sums.

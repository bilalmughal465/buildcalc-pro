How to calculate plywood sheathing

Sheathing is sold by the sheet, and the standard sheet is 4 ft by 8 ft, which is 32 sq ft. Add the floor area and the roof area, add waste, then divide by 32 and round up.

Formula: sheets = (floor area + roof area) × (1 + waste %) ÷ 32, rounded up

Worked example: 20 ft × 15 ft floor and roof

Floor: 20 × 15 = 300 sq ft. Roof: 20 × 15 = 300 sq ft. Total: 600 sq ft. Add 10% waste: 660 sq ft. Divide by 32: 20.63, rounded up to 21 sheets.

Roof slope

Measure the sloped roof area, not the flat footprint, or you will under-order. A steep roof can be much larger than its footprint.

Common sizes

Includes 10% waste. Sheets are 4x8 ft (32 sq ft). Cost uses $30 per sheet.

Cost tips

Prices on this page are editable estimates, not quotes. Get 2 to 3 local quotes before you buy, and ask if delivery is included. Delivery, tax and waste can change the total a lot. Order a little extra for cuts and breakage, and confirm final quantities with your supplier or a qualified professional.

Frequently asked questions

How many plywood sheets do I need for a 20x15 floor and roof?

A 20 ft by 15 ft floor plus the same roof is 600 sq ft, which needs 21 sheets of 4x8 plywood with 10% waste.

How big is a sheet of plywood?

The standard sheet is 4 ft by 8 ft, which is 32 sq ft.

What if I only need floor or roof?

Enter zero for the part you do not need. The calculator adds floor and roof areas together.

How much waste should I add?

Add 10% for rectangular areas and 15% for roofs with hips, valleys and dormers.

Are the prices accurate?

Prices are editable estimates. The defaults are typical US figures, and your local price can be higher or lower.

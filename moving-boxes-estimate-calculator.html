Moving Boxes Estimate Calculator

A typical room needs around 10–15 boxes. Multiply by your room count and add spares for the things you forgot.

—

Formula

Boxes = rooms x boxes per room, plus 10% spares.

How it works

Kitchens and book-filled rooms need far more boxes than bedrooms, so adjust the per-room figure up if you have lots of books or kitchenware.

Pack heavy things in small boxes and light things in large ones — a big box of books is nobody's friend on moving day.

FAQs

How many boxes does a kitchen need?
Often 15–20, since kitchenware is small, numerous and fragile.

Should I buy all boxes at once?
Buy the estimate plus spares in one go — running out mid-pack is the classic moving-day delay.

What sizes should I get?
A mix: small for heavy items, medium for most things, large only for light bulky items like bedding.

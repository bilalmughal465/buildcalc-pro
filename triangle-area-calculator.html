Triangle Area Calculator

Find the area of a triangle from its base and height.

—

Formula

Triangle area = ½ × base × height.

How it works

Enter the base and the vertical height. The calculator multiplies them and halves the result, since a triangle is half of a rectangle with the same base and height.

FAQs

It takes the values you enter and works out the triangle area for you, showing the result straight away.

What should I enter?
Enter ordinary numbers in the boxes above. If a value does not make sense for this calculation, the page will ask you to check it instead of showing a wrong answer.

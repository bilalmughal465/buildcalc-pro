Frequently asked questions

300 questions and answers about BuildCalc, its calculators and its tools.

What is BuildCalc?

BuildCalc is a free website with calculators and tools for construction, math, finance, health, cooking, pets and everyday life. You can use it in a browser, with no sign-up and no download.

Are BuildCalc calculators free?

Yes, all BuildCalc calculators and tools are free to use, with no sign-up and no download.

Do I need to sign up to use a calculator?

No, you do not need to sign up. Search for a calculator, enter your numbers, and get an instant result.

Are the results exact?

Results are estimates and general information only, based on the numbers you enter. See each calculator's own note for its limits.

How do I find the right calculator?

Use the search on the home page, or open a category box, such as Finance, Construction or Math, and pick a calculator from that list.

Does BuildCalc work on a phone?

Yes, every page is built to work on a phone browser, as well as on a computer.

Do I need to download an app?

No, there is no app to download. BuildCalc works in your browser.

Is my data saved by BuildCalc?

Most calculators work in your browser only and do not send your numbers to a server. Live tools that use a free external service are named on their own page and in the privacy policy.

What is the difference between a calculator and a tool?

A calculator gives a number from a formula, such as a loan payment. A tool helps with a task, such as a checklist, a timer, or a tracker, also in your browser.

Can I use BuildCalc in the USA and the UK?

Yes, BuildCalc is made for users in the USA, UK, Pakistan, Canada, Australia and around the world, with English pages throughout.

Why do some pages say estimates only?

Because a calculator uses the numbers you enter and a simple formula. Real costs, taxes and rates can differ, so always check with a professional for a final decision.

How do I share a calculator result?

On calculator pages you can use the Share button, or copy the page link from your browser and send it to someone else.

Can I print a calculator result?

Yes, use the Print result button on calculator pages, or your browser Print option.

What should I do if a calculator gives a strange result?

Check that you entered numbers in the right units, for example kilograms or pounds, and that no box is empty or negative where it should not be.

Does BuildCalc give financial advice?

No, BuildCalc gives estimates and general information only. It is not financial, legal or medical advice.

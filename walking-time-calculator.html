Walking Time Calculator

Work out how long a walk will take from distance and walking speed.

—

Formula

Walking time equals distance divided by speed, converted from hours to minutes.

How it works

Distance divided by speed gives hours, multiplied by 60 for minutes. Breaks, crossings and hills are not included, so add time for those on a real route.

FAQs

What is a normal walking speed?
About 5 km/h for a brisk adult walk; 4 km/h is leisurely.

Does it include rests?
No. Add planned rest time separately.

How do I estimate speed?
Time yourself over a known distance once.

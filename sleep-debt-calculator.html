Sleep Debt (Rough) Calculator

Add up the shortfall when nightly sleep falls below what you need, over several nights.

—

Formula

Sleep debt equals nightly shortfall (needed minus actual) multiplied by the number of nights.

How it works

The nightly gap is multiplied by the number of nights to give a cumulative shortfall. It is a rough bookkeeping figure, not a medical measure, and one long lie-in does not neatly repay it.

FAQs

Can I repay sleep debt in one weekend?
Only partly. Regular sleep beats occasional catch-up.

Is sleep debt real?
The feeling of accumulated tiredness is real, but this simple total is only an approximation.

What if I sleep more than needed?
Extra sleep is counted as zero debt here, not as credit.

General information only, not medical advice.

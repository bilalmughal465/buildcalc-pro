APR Estimate Calculator

Roughly estimate an APR from a loan amount, payment, and term.

—

Formula

APR is found by solving for the monthly rate that makes the present value of the payments equal the amount actually received, then annualising it.

How it works

This is a rough estimate found by iteration — it approximates the true APR, which also depends on fee timing and payment dates. Use a lender's disclosed APR for a final comparison.

FAQs

Why is APR higher than the interest rate?
Because fees are spread over the loan, raising the true yearly cost.

Why estimate instead of exact?
Exact APR needs payment dates and fee timing, which vary by lender.

What is the amount received?
Loan amount minus upfront fees — what actually reaches you.

Are these figures financial advice?
No — they are estimates for planning only, not financial advice. Check with a qualified adviser for decisions involving large sums.

Estimates only, not financial advice. Figures are approximate and do not include all fees, taxes, or changing rates unless stated.

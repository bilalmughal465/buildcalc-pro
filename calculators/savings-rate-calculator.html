Savings Rate Calculator

Find what share of income you save each month.

—

Formula

Savings rate equals amount saved divided by after-tax income, times 100.

How it works

Savings rate is one of the strongest predictors of financial progress, more than income alone. It counts money set aside for goals, investing, and debt overpayment in some definitions — here it is cash saved.

FAQs

What is a good savings rate?
20% is a common target; higher rates shorten the path to big goals.

Should debt repayment count?
Extra debt repayment builds net worth like saving, so some people include it.

Why after-tax income?
That is the money you actually control.

Are these figures financial advice?
No — they are estimates for planning only, not financial advice. Check with a qualified adviser for decisions involving large sums.

Tonnes to Pounds Converter

Convert tonnes to pounds instantly with a simple, free calculator.

Formula

Pounds = Tonnes × 2204.62. Multiply the value in tonnes by 2204.62 to get the value in pounds.

How it works

Enter a value in tonnes. The calculator multiplies it by the conversion factor and shows the equivalent in pounds, rounded to at most four decimal places so long decimals stay readable. Negative values are converted the same way, since the factor applies in both directions of the scale.

FAQs

How many pounds are in 1 tonnes?
One tonnes equals 2204.62 pounds. Multiply any value in tonnes by 2204.62 to get pounds.

Is this tonnes to pounds conversion exact?
One metric tonne is 1,000 kilograms, and one pound is 0.45359237 kilograms, giving about 2,204.62 pounds per tonne.

When would I need a tonnes to pounds conversion?
Any time a measurement, label, or instruction is given in tonnes and you need to work, compare, or record it in pounds — for example while shopping, travelling, cooking, studying, or planning a project.

Running Pace Calculator

Work out pace per kilometre and speed from a run's distance and time.

—

Formula

Pace equals total minutes divided by kilometres; speed equals kilometres divided by hours.

How it works

Total time is divided by distance for pace, and distance divided by time in hours for speed. Both describe the same run from opposite directions.

FAQs

What is a good pace?
It depends entirely on distance, fitness and goal; compare with your own past runs.

Why show both pace and speed?
Runners talk pace, cyclists and treadmills often show speed.

Does it include stops?
Only if you include stopped time in the total time entered.

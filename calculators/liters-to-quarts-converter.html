Liters to Quarts Converter

Convert liters to US quarts instantly with a simple, free calculator.

—

The result is the same volume restated in US quarts.

Formula

Us quarts = Liters × 1.05669. Multiply the value in liters by 1.05669 to get the value in US quarts.

How it works

Enter a value in liters. The calculator multiplies it by the conversion factor and shows the equivalent in US quarts, rounded to at most four decimal places so long decimals stay readable. Negative values are converted the same way, since the factor applies in both directions of the scale.

Worked example

For example, 5 liters × 1.05669 = 5.2834 US quarts.

FAQs

How many US quarts are in 1 liters?
One liters equals 1.05669 US quarts. Multiply any value in liters by 1.05669 to get US quarts.

Is this liters to US quarts conversion exact?
This uses the US liquid quart of 0.946353 liters. A UK quart is larger, at 1.13652 liters.

When would I need a liters to US quarts conversion?
Any time a measurement, label, or instruction is given in liters and you need to work, compare, or record it in US quarts — for example while shopping, travelling, cooking, studying, or planning a project.

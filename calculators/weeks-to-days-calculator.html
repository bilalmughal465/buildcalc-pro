Weeks to Days Calculator

Convert a number of weeks into days, and see the equivalent in hours too.

—

Formula

Days = weeks × 7, because every week has exactly seven days.

How it works

Enter a number of weeks. The calculator multiplies by 7 for days and by 168 for hours, which helps when planning leave, courses, or project phases measured in weeks.

FAQs

How many days are in a week?
Exactly 7 days.

Is a week always 7 days?
Yes, in the standard calendar week used for this conversion.

Can I enter half weeks?
Yes. Half a week is 3.5 days.

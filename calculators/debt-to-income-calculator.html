Debt-to-Income Ratio Calculator

Calculate DTI from monthly debt payments and gross monthly income.

—

Formula

DTI equals monthly debt payments divided by gross monthly income, times 100.

How it works

Lenders use DTI to judge affordability — lower is generally stronger. This uses gross income before tax, and debt payments include loans and cards, not rent in some definitions, so check which version a lender uses.

FAQs

What is a good DTI?
Under 36% is commonly seen as comfortable; above 43% can limit borrowing.

Does rent count?
Some lenders include housing costs, others use debt only — ask which they use.

Can I lower my DTI?
By paying down debt or raising income; the ratio moves with both.

Are these figures financial advice?
No — they are estimates for planning only, not financial advice. Check with a qualified adviser for decisions involving large sums.

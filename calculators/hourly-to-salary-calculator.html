Hourly to Salary Calculator

Convert an hourly wage into annual, monthly, and weekly pay.

—

Formula

Annual salary equals hourly wage times hours per week times weeks per year.

How it works

This assumes the same hours every week. Variable hours, overtime, and unpaid time off will change the real annual total.

FAQs

What about overtime?
Overtime is usually paid at a higher rate and is not included here.

Should I use 52 weeks?
Use your actual paid weeks if some time is unpaid.

Is this gross or net?
Gross — tax and deductions come off afterwards.

Are these figures financial advice?
No — they are estimates for planning only, not financial advice. Check with a qualified adviser for decisions involving large sums.

Party Food Quantity Calculator

Allow about 400 g of food per guest for a main meal, plus a 10% buffer so the table never looks empty.

—

Formula

Food = guests x food per guest, plus 10% buffer.

How it works

The per-guest figure depends on the meal: a light buffet needs less than a sit-down dinner, and teenagers at a party eat more than mixed-age guests.

Variety matters as much as quantity — several dishes in smaller amounts feel more generous than one huge dish.

FAQs

How much food per person for a buffet?
Around 300–400 g of main food per adult is a common planning figure, plus snacks and dessert.

Do children eat as much as adults?
No — children typically eat about half an adult portion, so adjust the guest count for young guests.

Is the 10% buffer enough?
For most parties, yes. If guests are big eaters or the party runs long, raise it to 20%.

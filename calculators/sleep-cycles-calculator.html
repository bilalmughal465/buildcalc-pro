Sleep Cycles Calculator

Turn a sleep duration into 90-minute sleep cycles and nearby cycle-friendly durations.

—

Formula

Sleep cycles equal total sleep hours divided by 1.5, since one cycle averages about 90 minutes.

How it works

Total sleep is divided by the average cycle length. Whole numbers of cycles are often suggested as waking points, because waking mid-cycle can feel groggier than waking between cycles.

FAQs

Is every cycle exactly 90 minutes?
No. Cycles vary from 70 to 120 minutes across the night.

Is 5 cycles enough?
For many adults 5 cycles (7.5 hours) is a reasonable target.

Does this diagnose sleep problems?
No. Persistent sleep trouble deserves proper advice.

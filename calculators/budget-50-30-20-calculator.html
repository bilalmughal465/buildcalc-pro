Budget 50/30/20 Calculator

Split after-tax income into needs, wants, and savings under the 50/30/20 rule.

—

Formula

Needs get 50% of income, wants 30%, and savings and debt repayment 20%.

How it works

This is a guideline, not a law — high housing costs can push needs above 50%, and the point is the balance, not hitting each number exactly. Adjust the split to your situation and track where money actually goes.

FAQs

What counts as a need?
Housing, food, utilities, transport to work, insurance, and minimum debt payments.

What counts as a want?
Dining out, streaming, holidays, and upgrades beyond the basics.

What if needs exceed 50%?
That is common in expensive cities — trim wants first, then look at housing over time.

Are these figures financial advice?
No — they are estimates for planning only, not financial advice. Check with a qualified adviser for decisions involving large sums.

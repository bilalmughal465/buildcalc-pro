Reorder Point Calculator

Find the stock level that should trigger a new order, using daily usage, lead time and safety stock.




—

Formula

Reorder point = (average daily usage multiplied by lead time in days) plus safety stock.

How it works

Multiply how many units sell each day by how many days a new order takes to arrive, then add safety stock for surprises. When stock falls to that level, order now: the remaining stock should last until the delivery lands.

FAQs

What is safety stock for?
It covers demand spikes and late deliveries, so a small delay does not empty the shelf.

What if lead time varies?
Use the longest realistic lead time, or add extra safety stock, to avoid stockouts.

How often should I recalculate?
Whenever sales pace, suppliers or lead times change noticeably.

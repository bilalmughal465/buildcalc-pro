Wedding Credit Card Monthly Interest Calculator (25 Years)

Estimate monthly interest on a card balance. This wedding version uses a 25-year planning term as its starting point.

—

This monthly interest figure is the cost of carrying the balance for one month at the entered rate, before any payment toward the balance itself, so paying only this amount leaves the balance unchanged and the same charge can repeat each month.

Formula

Monthly interest equals balance times annual rate divided by 100 divided by 12.

How it works

Paying only interest leaves the balance unchanged. Paying more than interest reduces the balance.

Worked example

Balance $5,000, annual rate 18%, term 25 years. Using the page formula, the monthly interest is $5,000 times 18 divided by 100 divided by 12, which gives $75.00 a month. That is the calculator's own result for these defaults, and it is the same on every page in this group because the term is not an input: only the balance and the rate are entered. Paying only that $75 of interest leaves the balance unchanged, so over 300 months the interest paid is 300 times $75, which is $22,500, and the $5,000 is still owed at the end. This total is an illustration only, assuming the rate stays at 18% and no payments beyond interest are made; it is not a second output of this page.

Who this term suits for a wedding: a couple approaching a silver anniversary still carrying wedding debt, a warning case: a balance that was never cleared has cost more than four times itself in interest, and the plan must now be to clear the capital, not just pay interest.

FAQs

Why is card interest high?
Card rates are usually higher than loan rates, so balances grow quickly if only minimum payments are made.

Is this wedding calculator exact?
No, it is an estimate for planning only, based on the numbers you enter.

Are these figures financial advice?
No, they are estimates for planning only, not financial advice. Check with a qualified adviser for decisions involving large sums.

Why is the 25-year total of $22,500 more than four times the balance?
Because $75 a month for 300 months is $22,500, and interest-only payments never reduce the $5,000. Over 25 years the interest alone is four and a half times the wedding spending it started from.

How should a couple review a 25-year wedding balance?
By looking at the total paid, not the monthly figure. If $22,500 has gone in interest and $5,000 is still owed, the right question is what fixed repayment clears the capital now, and what cheaper borrowing replaces the card.

What wedding risks grow over 25 years on a card?
Rate rises, income falls, illness, and new family costs all make a card balance harder to carry, while the interest keeps running. A 25-year card balance is exposed to every one of those risks for a working lifetime.

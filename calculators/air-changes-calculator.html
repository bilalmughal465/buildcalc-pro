Air Changes Calculator

Work out air changes per hour from room volume and ventilation airflow.



—

Formula

Air changes per hour = airflow in cubic metres per hour divided by room volume in cubic metres.

How it works

Divide the ventilation system's hourly airflow by the room's volume. The result says how many times the room's air is replaced each hour. Kitchens and bathrooms usually need more changes than bedrooms or living rooms.

FAQs

What is a typical ACH?
Living spaces often sit around 0.5 to 1, while kitchens and bathrooms need several changes per hour.

Is higher always better?
More fresh air helps air quality but costs more to heat or cool, so balance comfort, health and energy.

Does opening a window count?
Natural ventilation varies with wind and temperature, so this calculation suits mechanical airflow figures best.

Home Cleaning Time Calculator

Multiply rooms by a typical minutes-per-room figure to plan a cleaning session honestly.

—

Formula

Cleaning time = rooms x minutes per room.

How it works

Bathrooms and kitchens take longer than bedrooms because of fixtures and grease; adjust the per-room figure up if your home has several of those.

A deep clean takes roughly twice a regular tidy — if you are spring cleaning, double the estimate.

FAQs

How long does a bathroom take?
A regular clean is about 20–30 minutes; a deep clean with descaling can take twice that.

Does tidying count separately?
Yes. Decluttering before cleaning saves time overall, since cleaning around clutter is slow.

Can two people halve the time?
Roughly, yes — two people in different rooms finish in about half the time, with small losses to coordination.

Discount Price Calculator

Work out the sale price and amount saved from a discount percentage.

—

Formula

Sale price equals original price times one minus discount divided by 100.

How it works

Enter the list price and the discount. The calculator shows both what you pay and what you save, which helps compare deals across stores.

FAQs

Is the discount applied before tax?
Usually yes — tax is then charged on the discounted price.

Can I stack two discounts?
Apply them one after another, not by adding the percentages.

Why check the saving too?
A big percentage on a small price can save less than a small percentage on a big price.

Are these figures financial advice?
No — they are estimates for planning only, not financial advice. Check with a qualified adviser for decisions involving large sums.

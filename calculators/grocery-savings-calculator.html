Grocery Savings Calculator

Enter the original price, the sale price and how many you are buying to see the real saving.

—

Formula

Saving per item = original price − sale price. Total saving = saving per item x quantity.

How it works

A percentage off only matters against the price you would actually pay. A 50% saving on something you did not need is still money spent.

Stocking up makes sense for non-perishables you use regularly; for fresh food, only buy what you will eat before it spoils.

FAQs

Is a bigger percentage always a better deal?
No. Compare the final price with other brands too — a small discount on a cheap item can beat a big discount on a pricey one.

Should I buy extra just because it is on sale?
Only if you will use it in time. Extra perishables that spoil cost more than they save.

How do I compare two different sales?
Compare the final price per unit, not the discount percentage — that is the number you actually pay.

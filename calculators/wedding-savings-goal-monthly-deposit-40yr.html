Wedding Savings Goal Monthly Deposit Calculator (40 Years)

Find the monthly deposit needed to reach a savings goal. This wedding version uses a 40-year planning term as its starting point.

—

Formula

Monthly deposit equals goal times monthly rate divided by one plus monthly rate to the power of months minus one.

How it works

Saving a fixed amount each month with interest reaches the goal faster than saving without interest.

Worked example

Goal amount $50,000, annual rate 5%, term 40 years. The monthly rate is 5% divided by 12, which is about 0.4167% a month, and the number of deposits is 40 times 12, which is 480 months. Using the page formula, the monthly deposit is $50,000 times the monthly rate divided by one plus the monthly rate to the power of 480, minus one, which gives $32.76. That is the calculator output for these defaults. Over 480 months the couple pays in $15,727.18 in total, so $34,272.82 of the $50,000 goal comes from interest. Totals use the calculator's unrounded figure, so the rounded monthly amount times 480 can differ by a dollar or two.

Who this term suits for a wedding: nobody as an ordinary wedding plan, because a 40-year horizon is a whole working life, where $32.76 a month is useful only as a warning illustration that a small steady habit compounds into a very large figure, and any real wedding fund must be reviewed often.

FAQs

How much should I save each month?
Divide the goal by the growth of regular deposits at the assumed rate to get the monthly amount.

Is this wedding calculator exact?
No, it is an estimate for planning only, based on the numbers you enter.

Are these figures financial advice?
No, they are estimates for planning only, not financial advice. Check with a qualified adviser for decisions involving large sums.

Is $32.76 a month for 40 years a realistic wedding plan?
No, not as a real plan. 480 deposits of $32.76 pay in $15,727.18, and interest adds $34,272.82 to reach $50,000, but a 40-year horizon is a whole working life: wedding prices, styles and the couple's plans will all change many times.

What does $50 a month reach over 40 years at 5%?
$50 a month for 480 months reaches $76,301.01, which is $26,301.01 more than the $50,000 goal. Even a small habit compounds into a large figure over 40 years.

What should a couple check before trusting a 40-year wedding figure?
Everything that changes over 40 years: the rate will not stay at 5%, the $50,000 goal will not stay fixed, and deposits will not stay at $32.76. Review the plan every few years and reset the goal as the wedding gets closer.

Cycling Calories Burned Calculator

Estimate cycling energy use from weight, time and riding effort.

—

Formula

Calories equal MET value × weight in kilograms × hours of cycling.

How it works

Effort sets the MET value, time converts to hours, and weight scales the total. Hills and wind change real effort a lot, so a flat-road estimate will understate a hilly ride.

FAQs

Does bike weight matter?
A little, but rider weight dominates the calculation.

Why do estimates vary so much?
Speed, hills and wind are not in this simple formula.

Is outdoor different from indoor?
Indoor trainers remove wind and hills, so effort bands differ.

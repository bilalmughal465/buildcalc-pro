Travel Credit Card Monthly Interest Calculator (20 Years)

Estimate monthly interest on a card balance. This travel version uses a 20-year planning term as its starting point.

—

Formula

Monthly interest equals balance times annual rate divided by 100 divided by 12.

How it works

Paying only interest leaves the balance unchanged. Paying more than interest reduces the balance.

Worked example

Balance $5,000, annual rate 18 percent, term 20 years. Using the page formula, the monthly interest is $5,000 times 18 divided by 100 divided by 12, which is $75.00 a month. That is the calculator's own result for these defaults, and it is the same on every page in this group because the term is not an input: only the balance and the rate decide the answer. Paying only $75.00 a month leaves the $5,000 balance unchanged, so over 20 years (240 months) of interest-only payments the total interest paid would be $18,000, and the $5,000 would still be owed at the end. That total is an illustration only, not a second output of this page.

Who this term suits for a traveller: a traveller with a long-standing card balance from years of travel, where the question is no longer the trip itself but whether the balance should move to a cheaper loan.

FAQs

Why is card interest high?
Card rates are usually higher than loan rates, so balances grow quickly if only minimum payments are made.

Is this travel calculator exact?
No, it is an estimate for planning only, based on the numbers you enter.

Are these figures financial advice?
No, they are estimates for planning only, not financial advice. Check with a qualified adviser for decisions involving large sums.

Is $75 a month for 20 years realistic for a traveller?
Only as a warning figure. Twenty years of interest-only payments costs $18,000 and leaves the $5,000 still owed, which no travel plan should accept.

What if new trips keep being added to the same card?
The balance grows, the monthly interest rises above $75.00, and the total interest over 20 years grows well beyond $18,000.

Should a 20-year card balance be moved to a cheaper travel loan?
Usually yes. A loan with a lower rate and a fixed end date costs less than 20 years of card interest, but the new loan must still be repaid on time.

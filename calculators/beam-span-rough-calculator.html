Beam Span Rough Calculator

Get a very rough beam depth guide from span length. This is not a structural design.


—

Formula

Rough depth guide = span in millimetres divided by 20. This is a rule-of-thumb starting point only, not a design calculation.

How it works

Enter the distance the beam must span. The guide divides span by twenty to suggest an approximate depth for early planning. Loads, material, supports and deflection all change the real answer, so a licensed engineer must design any beam that carries real load.

FAQs

Can I build from this guide?
No. This is only for early planning and budgeting. Real beams need a proper structural design.

Why span divided by twenty?
It is a common rough rule for initial sizing. Different beam types and loads use different ratios.

What else affects beam size?
Load, material, support conditions, deflection limits and local codes all matter, which is why professional design is required.

Rough estimate only — consult a licensed professional for structural work before building. Structural work must be designed by a licensed professional.

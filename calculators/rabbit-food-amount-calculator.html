Rabbit Food Amount Calculator

Estimate daily pellets and vegetables for a rabbit from body weight, with unlimited hay.

—

Formula

Pellets are about 12.5 g per kilogram of body weight per day; vegetables about half a cup per kilogram.

How it works

Weight scales both pellets and vegetables, while hay stays unlimited because it drives digestion and dental wear. Introduce new vegetables gradually, one at a time.

FAQs

What is the most important food?
Hay. It should make up most of the diet.

Can rabbits eat unlimited pellets?
No. Too many pellets crowd out hay.

Are all vegetables safe?
No. Some are unsuitable, so check each new vegetable first.

Sales Tax Calculator

Add sales tax to a price, or extract the tax already included in a price.

—

The result shows how much of the amount is sales tax, so the other figure is the price before tax or the total to pay, depending on the option chosen.

Formula

For a tax-exclusive price, total equals price times one plus rate divided by 100; for a tax-inclusive price, the tax part is price minus price divided by one plus rate divided by 100.

How it works

Rates vary by place and product, so enter the rate that applies to you — no rate is assumed here. The toggle lets you work in either direction.

Worked example

For example, with a price of $100, a sales tax rate of 8%, and the price not already including tax, the tax is $8.00 and the total is $108.00.

FAQs

Where do I find my rate?
From your local tax authority or a receipt from a recent purchase.

Why do rates differ?
Different states, cities, and product categories set different rates.

Does this cover VAT?
The same math applies to VAT, but rules and rates differ by country.

Are these figures financial advice?
No — they are estimates for planning only, not financial advice. Check with a qualified adviser for decisions involving large sums.

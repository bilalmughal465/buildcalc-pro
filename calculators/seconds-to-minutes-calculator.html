Seconds to Minutes Calculator

Convert a number of seconds into minutes and seconds.

—

Formula

Minutes = whole seconds divided by 60, rounded down; remaining seconds = the remainder.

How it works

Enter total seconds. The calculator splits them into whole minutes plus leftover seconds, useful for race times, video lengths, and timed tasks.

FAQs

How many seconds are in a minute?
Exactly 60 seconds.

What about 90 seconds?
90 seconds equals 1 minute 30 seconds, or 1.50 minutes.

Can I enter fractional seconds?
Yes. The result rounds the leftover seconds to the nearest whole second.

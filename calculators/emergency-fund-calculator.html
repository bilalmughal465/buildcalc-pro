Emergency Fund Calculator

Size an emergency fund from monthly essential expenses and months of cover.

—

Formula

Emergency fund equals monthly essential expenses times months of cover.

How it works

Essential expenses mean housing, food, utilities, transport, and insurance — not luxuries. Three to six months is a common target; single-income households often aim higher.

FAQs

Why only essentials?
In an emergency you cut discretionary spending, so the fund covers the basics.

Where should the fund sit?
In an accessible savings account, not in investments that can fall.

Should I build it before investing?
Most advisers say yes — a buffer stops emergencies becoming debt.

Are these figures financial advice?
No — they are estimates for planning only, not financial advice. Check with a qualified adviser for decisions involving large sums.

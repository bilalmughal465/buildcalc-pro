Grams to Ounces Converter

Convert grams to ounces instantly with a simple, free calculator.

Formula

Ounces = Grams × 0.035274. Multiply the value in grams by 0.035274 to get the value in ounces.

How it works

Enter a value in grams. The calculator multiplies it by the conversion factor and shows the equivalent in ounces, rounded to at most four decimal places so long decimals stay readable. Negative values are converted the same way, since the factor applies in both directions of the scale.

FAQs

How many ounces are in 1 grams?
One grams equals 0.035274 ounces. Multiply any value in grams by 0.035274 to get ounces.

Is this grams to ounces conversion exact?
One avoirdupois ounce equals exactly 28.349523125 grams, so the conversion is exact before rounding.

When would I need a grams to ounces conversion?
Any time a measurement, label, or instruction is given in grams and you need to work, compare, or record it in ounces — for example while shopping, travelling, cooking, studying, or planning a project.

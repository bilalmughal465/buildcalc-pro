BMI Prime Calculator

Express BMI as a ratio to the upper healthy limit of 25, so 1.0 marks that boundary.

—

Formula

BMI Prime equals BMI divided by 25, the upper end of the usual healthy BMI range.

How it works

The page first works out BMI from weight and height, then divides it by 25. A value of 1.0 sits exactly on that boundary, below 1.0 is under it, and above 1.0 is over it.

FAQs

Is BMI Prime better than BMI?
It is the same information rescaled, so it adds clarity, not new accuracy.

What does 1.0 mean?
It means a BMI of 25, the top of the usual healthy range.

Does it work for athletes?
No better than BMI itself; muscle still raises the number.

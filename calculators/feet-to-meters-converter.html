Feet to Meters Converter

Convert feet to meters instantly with a simple, free calculator.

Formula

Meters = Feet × 0.3048. Multiply the value in feet by 0.3048 to get the value in meters.

How it works

Enter a value in feet. The calculator multiplies it by the conversion factor and shows the equivalent in meters, rounded to at most four decimal places so long decimals stay readable. Negative values are converted the same way, since the factor applies in both directions of the scale.

FAQs

How many meters are in 1 feet?
One feet equals 0.3048 meters. Multiply any value in feet by 0.3048 to get meters.

Is this feet to meters conversion exact?
One foot is defined as exactly 0.3048 meters, so the conversion is exact before rounding.

When would I need a feet to meters conversion?
Any time a measurement, label, or instruction is given in feet and you need to work, compare, or record it in meters — for example while shopping, travelling, cooking, studying, or planning a project.

Fibonacci Calculator

Find the nth Fibonacci number, where each term is the sum of the two before it.

—

This result is the Fibonacci number at the position entered, counting from 0, so it shows the term in the sequence that the entered position points to.

Formula

The Fibonacci sequence starts 0, 1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, and each later term is the sum of the two terms before it.

How it works

Enter n from 0 to 100, counting from 0. The calculator builds the sequence step by step. Fibonacci(10) is 55.

FAQs

What does the Fibonacci do?
It takes the values you enter and works out the fibonacci for you, showing the result straight away.

What should I enter?
Enter ordinary numbers in the boxes above. If a value does not make sense for this calculation, the page will ask you to check it instead of showing a wrong answer.

Can I use the result for homework or everyday math?
Yes. It is a general-purpose math helper; for exam or professional work, still check the steps against your own working.

Book Reading Time Calculator

Multiply pages by your minutes per page, then divide by your daily reading time to see the finish date in days.

—

Formula

Total minutes = pages x minutes per page. Days = total minutes / daily reading minutes.

How it works

Minutes per page varies with the book: light fiction runs faster than dense non-fiction, and your pace improves as you settle into a book.

A small daily habit beats occasional long sessions for most readers — thirty minutes a day finishes a 300-page book in about three weeks.

FAQs

What is a typical reading speed?
Around 200–250 words per minute for most adults, which is roughly 1–2 minutes per average page.

Why does non-fiction take longer?
Denser ideas, unfamiliar terms and the need to pause and think all slow the pace compared with a novel.

Should I count re-reading?
For a first read, no. If you re-read passages often, add a little to your minutes-per-page figure.

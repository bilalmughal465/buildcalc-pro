Startup Present Value Calculator (25 Years)

Find how much a future amount is worth today. This startup version uses a 25-year planning term as its starting point.

—

The Present value is what a future amount is worth today after 25 years of discounting, so a lower figure means the long wait has taken more value away in today's money, not that the future amount itself has changed.

Formula

Present value equals future amount divided by one plus rate to the power of years.

How it works

A future amount is worth less today because money today can earn interest.

Worked example

Future amount $50,000, annual rate 5 percent, term 25 years. Using the page formula, present value equals $50,000 divided by 1.05 to the power of 25, which is $14,765.14. That is the calculator's own result for these defaults. The discount for waiting is $50,000 minus $14,765.14, which is $35,234.86.

Who this term suits for a startup founder: a founder doing succession or long-range planning, such as valuing a stake or royalty stream about 25 years out, where the future sum keeps less than a third of its value today.

FAQs

What is present value?
Present value is what a future sum is worth today after allowing for interest over time.

Is this startup calculator exact?
No, it is an estimate for planning only, based on the numbers you enter.

Are these figures financial advice?
No, they are estimates for planning only, not financial advice. Check with a qualified adviser for decisions involving large sums.

Why does $50,000 in 25 years keep less than a third of its value today?
Because 1.05 to the power of 25 is about 3.386, so $50,000 divided by 3.386 is $14,765.14. The discount of $35,234.86 is more than twice the present value itself.

Is a 25-year startup figure useful when most startups change fast?
Only as a sensitivity check. No startup plan survives 25 years unchanged, so use $14,765.14 to show how much waiting costs, then re-plan on a shorter, realistic horizon.

Should a founder accept $15,000 now instead of $50,000 in 25 years?
At 5 percent, yes, roughly: $14,765.14 today grows to $50,000 in 25 years. But if your startup earns more than 5 percent, or the future payment is uncertain, the cash now is worth even more.

Travel Loan EMI Calculator (10 Years)

Estimate the monthly EMI for a loan from amount, rate and term. This travel version uses a 10-year planning term as its starting point.

—

Formula

EMI equals principal times monthly rate times one plus monthly rate to the power of payments, divided by that power minus one.

How it works

This assumes a fixed rate and equal monthly payments with no extra fees.

Worked example

Loan amount 200,000 dollars, annual rate 6.5 percent, term 10 years. The monthly rate is 6.5 percent divided by 12, which is about 0.5417 percent a month, and the number of payments is 10 times 12, which is 120 months. Using the page formula, the monthly EMI is 2,270.96 dollars. That is the calculator output for these defaults. Over the full term the total paid is 272,515.15 dollars, so the total interest is 272,515.15 dollars minus 200,000 dollars, which is 72,515.15 dollars.

Who this term suits for a traveller: a traveller starting a small travel business, such as a tour van, a guesthouse deposit, or a travel agency fit-out, where the loan is paid from takings over a decade.

FAQs

What is an EMI?
An EMI is a fixed monthly payment that covers both interest and principal over the loan term.

Is this travel calculator exact?
No, it is an estimate for planning only, based on the numbers you enter.

Are these figures financial advice?
No, they are estimates for planning only, not financial advice. Check with a qualified adviser for decisions involving large sums.

Is a 2,270.96 dollar EMI easier for a traveller than 3,913.23 dollars?
Yes, month by month it is much lighter, which suits seasonal travel income. The trade-off is total interest of 72,515.15 dollars over 10 years, more than double the 5-year figure of 34,793.78 dollars, because the loan runs twice as long.

Why does 10-year interest cost more than double the 5-year interest?
Because interest is charged on the balance every month, and a longer term keeps a larger balance alive for longer. Ten years means 120 payments of 2,270.96 dollars, giving 272,515.15 dollars in total, so interest is 72,515.15 dollars.

What if the traveller borrows only 100,000 dollars for 10 years?
At the same 6.5 percent rate, the EMI would be 1,135.48 dollars a month, exactly half of 2,270.96 dollars, because halving the loan halves every payment. A smaller loan is the simplest way to make a travel plan affordable.

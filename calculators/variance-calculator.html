Variance Calculator

Find the variance of a list of numbers, the average squared distance of each value from the mean.

—

The result is the population variance of the numbers entered, so a larger value means the numbers are more spread out around their mean.

Formula

Population variance = average of (each value − mean) squared.

How it works

Type numbers separated by commas. The calculator finds the mean, squares each difference from it, and averages those squares.

Worked example

For example, the numbers 4, 8, 15, 16, 23 and 42 have a mean of 18, the squared differences from the mean add to 910, and 910 divided by 6 gives a population variance of 151.6667.

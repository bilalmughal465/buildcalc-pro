Formula

Portions = batch weight / portion size. Freezer life depends on food type.

How it works

Freezer life is about quality, not safety: frozen food stays safe almost indefinitely, but texture and flavour fade over months.

Flat freezer bags freeze faster and stack better than bulky tubs, and labelling the date stops mystery meals.

FAQs

Is old frozen food unsafe?
Not usually — freezing pauses bacterial growth. But after months, taste and texture decline noticeably.

Can I refreeze thawed food?
Only if it was thawed in the fridge and cooked first. Repeated thawing hurts quality and can be risky.

Why label portions?
Because after a month in the freezer, most dishes look identical. A date and a name save guesswork.

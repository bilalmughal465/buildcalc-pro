Lease Payment Estimate Calculator

Roughly estimate a monthly lease payment from price, residual, and money factor.

—

Formula

Monthly lease payment is depreciation (price minus residual over term) plus finance charge (price plus residual times money factor).

How it works

Money factor is the lease's interest expression — multiply by 2,400 for a rough APR. Taxes, fees, and mileage charges are not included.

FAQs

What is residual value?
The expected value at lease end, set by the leasing company.

How do I get APR from money factor?
Multiply money factor by 2,400 for an approximate APR.

Why lease instead of buy?
Lower monthly payments and a newer car, but no ownership at the end.

Are these figures financial advice?
No — they are estimates for planning only, not financial advice. Check with a qualified adviser for decisions involving large sums.

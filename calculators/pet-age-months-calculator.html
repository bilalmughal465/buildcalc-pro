Pet Age in Months Calculator

Convert a pet's age in years and months into total months.

—

Formula

Total months equal years multiplied by 12, plus extra months.

How it works

Years are converted to months and the remainder added. Vets, insurers and food guides often ask for age in months for young animals, where a few months matter.

FAQs

Why do forms ask months?
For puppies and kittens, growth stages change month by month.

What if I only know years?
Enter extra months as 0.

Does this convert to human years?
No. Use the dog or cat human-age calculators for that.

General information only, not veterinary advice.

Shift Length Calculator

Find the length of a work shift, including an unpaid break, with overnight shifts handled.

—

Formula

Gross shift = end minus start, wrapping past midnight for overnight shifts. Net shift = gross shift minus break minutes.

How it works

Pick the shift start and end times and enter the unpaid break in minutes. The calculator reports both gross and net length, with overnight shifts treated as ending the next day.

FAQs

What if the shift runs overnight?
An end time earlier than the start is read as the next day, so 22:00 to 06:00 is 8 hours.

Is the break paid or unpaid here?
Unpaid: it is subtracted from the gross length to give net working time.

Can the break exceed the shift?
The net result is floored at zero, so an impossible break gives 0 net hours rather than a negative shift.

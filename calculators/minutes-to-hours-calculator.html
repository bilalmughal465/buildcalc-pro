Minutes to Hours Calculator

Convert a number of minutes into hours and minutes.

—

Formula

Hours = whole minutes divided by 60, rounded down; remaining minutes = the remainder after that division.

How it works

Enter total minutes. The calculator splits them into whole hours plus leftover minutes and also shows the decimal-hour equivalent, so 150 minutes reads as 2 hours 30 minutes, or 2.50 hours.

FAQs

How many minutes are in an hour?
Exactly 60 minutes.

Why show decimal hours too?
Because timesheets and billing often use decimal hours, and the two forms are easy to confuse.

Do negative minutes work?
The arithmetic still applies, but negative durations are unusual; check the sign of your input if the result looks odd.

Home Loan Total Interest Calculator (25 Years)

Estimate total interest paid over a loan. This home version uses a 25-year planning term as its starting point.

—

Over 25 years, total interest is the extra amount paid to the lender on top of the borrowed amount, and total paid is the borrowed amount plus that interest, so together they show the full cost of the home loan rather than just the price of the home.

Formula

Total interest equals monthly EMI times number of payments minus the principal.

How it works

A longer term lowers the monthly payment but raises total interest.

Worked example

Loan amount $200,000, annual rate 6.5%, term 25 years. The monthly rate is 6.5% divided by 12, which is 0.0054167, and there are 300 monthly payments. Using the page formula, the EMI is $1,350.41 a month, so the total paid is $405,124.30. Total interest is the total paid minus the principal, which is $405,124.30 minus $200,000, equal to $205,124.30, or 102.6 of the amount borrowed.

At 25 years total interest passes the amount borrowed: $205,124.30 of interest on a $200,000 home loan. For a home buyer this is the warning line in the group — beyond it, the household pays more in interest than it borrowed for the home itself.

Who this term suits for a home buyer: a home buyer taking the term at which interest first passes the amount borrowed, for example a family buying a long-term home who must be sure the monthly payment leaves room for maintenance, insurance, and rate rises, because the lifetime bill is already over $200,000 in interest alone.

FAQs

Why does a longer loan cost more interest?
More payments mean interest is charged for longer, so total interest rises even if each payment is smaller.

Is this home calculator exact?
No, it is an estimate for planning only, based on the numbers you enter.

Are these figures financial advice?
No, they are estimates for planning only, not financial advice. Check with a qualified adviser for decisions involving large sums.

Why does interest pass the amount borrowed at 25 years?
Because 300 payments give interest 25 years to build up. At $1,350.41 a month the total paid is $405,124.30, so interest alone is $205,124.30, more than the $200,000 borrowed for the home.

Is a 25-year home loan ever sensible?
Yes, it is a common term for buyers who need a lower monthly payment to afford a home at all. It is sensible only if the $1,350.41 payment leaves room for maintenance, insurance, and rate rises, and if overpayments are planned when income grows.

How do overpayments after a pay rise shorten a 25-year home loan?
Every overpayment cuts the balance, so less interest is charged in later months and the loan finishes earlier than 300 payments. On a 25-year loan, regular overpayments can save a large share of the $205,124.30 interest bill.

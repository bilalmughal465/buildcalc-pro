Sales Growth Rate Calculator

Measure sales growth between two periods as a percentage change.



—

Formula

Sales growth rate = (current period sales minus previous period sales) divided by previous period sales, multiplied by 100.

How it works

Put last period's sales beside this period's sales, using equal-length periods such as two months or two years. A positive percentage means growth, a negative one means sales shrank. Comparing equal periods avoids seasonal distortion.

FAQs

Should I compare months or years?
Either, as long as both periods are the same length. Year-on-year comparisons smooth out seasonal swings.

What if previous sales were zero?
Growth cannot be expressed as a percentage from zero, so this calculator asks for a positive starting figure.

Does growth mean profit grew too?
Not necessarily. Sales can grow while profit falls if costs grew faster.

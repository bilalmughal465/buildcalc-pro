Business Inflation Adjusted Value Calculator (25 Years)

Estimate what an amount will be worth after inflation. This business version uses a 25-year planning term as its starting point.

—

In real terms, the Future amount is the sum you would need after 25 years at the entered inflation rate to buy the same things that the original amount buys today.

Formula

Future amount equals amount times one plus inflation rate to the power of years.

How it works

Inflation means the same amount buys less over time, so future prices are higher.

Worked example

Amount $10,000, inflation rate 3%, term 25 years. The growth factor is 1.03 to the power of 25, which is 2.093778. The future amount is $10,000 times 2.093778, which is $20,937.78. That is $10,937.78 more than today, a rise of 109.4%. A basket of business costs that is $10,000 today would need $20,937.78 after 25 years at a steady 3% inflation to buy the same things. After 25 years the amount has more than doubled, so the rise itself is now larger than the original sum.

Who this term suits for a business: a family business planning a handover to the next generation, where the value of stock, premises, and working capital in 25 years must be judged in future money before any handover price or share split is agreed.

FAQs

What does inflation do to money?
Inflation reduces what money can buy, so a sum today will need to be larger in future to buy the same things.

Is this business calculator exact?
No, it is an estimate for planning only, based on the numbers you enter.

Are these figures financial advice?
No, they are estimates for planning only, not financial advice. Check with a qualified adviser for decisions involving large sums.

Why does the amount more than double by 25 years?
Because 1.03 to the power of 25 is 2.093778, just over 2. Doubling at 3% takes about 23 to 24 years, so by year 25 the amount has passed double. The rise of $10,937.78 is larger than the starting $10,000, which surprises many owners who think of 3% as small.

How does a 25-year inflation figure change a family business handover plan?
A handover price fixed in today's money will look far too low in 25 years. If the business is worth $10,000 of today's costs now, the same real value needs about $20,937.78 then. Agree how the price will be adjusted, or the next generation may inherit a business priced for a different era.

What if inflation averages 4% instead of 3% over 25 years?
The future amount would be $26,658.36, not $20,937.78, because 1.04 to the power of 25 is about 2.665836. A single extra point of inflation adds about $5,720.58 over 25 years on every $10,000, which is why long plans should always be stress-tested at a higher rate.

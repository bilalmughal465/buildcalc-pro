Formula

Tip = bill x tip % / 100. Total = bill + tip.

How it works

Tipping customs vary by country and service type, but 10–20% is common in restaurants in many places where tipping is expected.

If the bill already includes a service charge, check before adding a tip on top — you may already have paid it.

FAQs

Should I tip on the pre-tax amount?
Common practice is to tip on the pre-tax subtotal, though many people tip on the total for simplicity.

What is a standard restaurant tip?
Around 15–20% for good table service in places where tipping is customary.

Do I tip for takeaway?
It is optional and usually smaller — a few percent or a round-up is common.

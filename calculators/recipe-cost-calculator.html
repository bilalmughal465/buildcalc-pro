Cooking & Kitchen

Recipe Cost Calculator

Enter the cost of up to three main ingredients and the servings the recipe makes, to see the total and per-serving cost.

—

Formula

Total cost = sum of ingredient costs. Per serving = total / servings.

How it works

For most home recipes, three or four main ingredients make up nearly all the cost; pantry staples like salt and oil add only pennies.

Costing recipes this way quickly shows which meals are budget-friendly and which only look cheap because the expensive ingredient was forgotten.

FAQs

Should I include pantry staples?
You can, but they rarely change the total much. Include them only if you want a precise figure.

What if I use only part of a pack?
Enter the cost of the part you used, not the whole pack — for example, half a pack at half its price.

How do I cost a recipe with many ingredients?
Group small ones together as one 'other ingredients' amount to keep it simple.

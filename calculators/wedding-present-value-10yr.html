Wedding Present Value Calculator (10 Years)

Find how much a future amount is worth today. This wedding version uses a 10-year planning term as its starting point.

—

The Present value figure is what the future wedding amount is worth in today's money after discounting it at the rate entered over 10 years, so it shows how much would need to be set aside today to match that future amount at that rate.

Formula

Present value equals future amount divided by one plus rate to the power of years.

How it works

A future amount is worth less today because money today can earn interest.

Worked example

Future amount $50,000, annual rate 5 percent, term 10 years. Using the page formula, the present value is $50,000 divided by 1.05 to the power of 10, which is $50,000 divided by 1.6289, giving $30,695.66. That is the calculator output for these defaults. The discount is $50,000 minus $30,695.66, which is $19,304.34, so the future wedding is worth 61.39 percent of its future amount in today's money.

Wedding use case: A couple planning a wedding about 10 years away, or parents starting a wedding fund for a young child, who want a realistic today's-money figure for a $50,000 celebration rather than guessing from future prices.

FAQs

What is present value?
Present value is what a future sum is worth today after allowing for interest over time.

Is this wedding calculator exact?
No, it is an estimate for planning only, based on the numbers you enter.

Are these figures financial advice?
No, they are estimates for planning only, not financial advice. Check with a qualified adviser for decisions involving large sums.

Why is a $50,000 wedding worth only $30,695.66 today over 10 years?
Because 10 years of discounting at 5 percent divides $50,000 by 1.6289, leaving 61.39 percent of the future amount. The $19,304.34 discount is the price of waiting 10 years at that rate.

Is 10 years too long to plan a wedding budget?
Ten years is long enough that prices, venues and tastes will change, so treat $30,695.66 as a starting target, not a final budget. Revisit the figure every year or two as the wedding gets closer.

What if the wedding budget is $40,000 instead of $50,000?
At 5 percent over 10 years, a $40,000 future wedding is worth $24,556.53 today. Present value scales directly with the future amount, so a smaller wedding needs proportionally less set aside today.

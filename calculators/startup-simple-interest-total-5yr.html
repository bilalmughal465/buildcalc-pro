Startup Simple Interest Total Calculator (5 Years)

Calculate simple interest and the total amount to repay. This startup version uses a 5-year planning term as its starting point.

—

In real terms, the Interest figure is the extra cost of borrowing beyond the original $10,000, and the Total figure is the full amount to repay after 5 years, so a short term keeps the extra cost modest compared with a longer loan at the same rate.

Formula

Simple interest equals principal times rate times time, divided by 100.

How it works

Interest is charged only on the original principal, not on interest already added.

Worked example

Principal $10,000, annual rate 5 percent, term 5 years. Using the page formula, simple interest equals $10,000 times 5 times 5, divided by 100, which is $2,500.00. The total to repay is $10,000 plus $2,500.00, which is $12,500.00. That is the calculator's own result for these defaults.

That yearly figure never changes: 5 percent of $10,000 is $500 every year, so over 5 years the interest is 25 percent of the amount borrowed.

Who this term suits for a startup founder: a new startup borrowing for launch costs, such as a first product run, laptops for a small team, or a modest office fit-out, where early revenue should clear the loan within about 5 years.

FAQs

What is simple interest?
Simple interest is interest charged only on the original amount, not on accumulated interest.

Is this startup calculator exact?
No, it is an estimate for planning only, based on the numbers you enter.

Are these figures financial advice?
No, they are estimates for planning only, not financial advice. Check with a qualified adviser for decisions involving large sums.

Is $2,500 of interest a lot on a $10,000 startup loan over 5 years?
It is $500 a year, which is 25 percent of the amount borrowed over 5 years. Whether it is a lot depends on what the loan earns: a first product run or launch stock that brings in more than $12,500 covers it, while a loan spent on costs that earn nothing does not.

Why is the interest exactly $500 a year here?
Because 5 percent of $10,000 is $500, and simple interest is charged only on the original principal. It never grows from year to year, unlike compound interest, so every year costs the same $500.

What if the startup repays early, after 3 years?
At $500 a year, 3 years of interest is $1,500 and the total is $11,500, which saves $1,000 compared with the 5-year figure. Check first whether the lender charges an early-repayment fee, which is not counted here.

Wedding EMI Affordability Calculator (20 Years)

Estimate a safe monthly EMI from income. This wedding version uses a 20-year planning term as its starting point.

—

The safe monthly EMI is the largest monthly payment that stays within the chosen share of income, leaving the rest of your income for living costs and other debts.

Formula

Safe EMI equals monthly income times safe share divided by 100.

How it works

Lenders often use a share of income as a guide, but other debts also matter.

Worked example

Monthly income $5,000, safe EMI share 30 percent, term 20 years. Using the page formula, the safe EMI is $5,000 times 30 divided by 100, which gives $1,500 a month. That is the calculator's own result for these defaults, and it is the same on every page in this group because the term is not an input: only the income and the safe share are entered. Against a 20-year planning window of 240 months, $1,500 a month adds up to $360,000 in total EMI capacity, as a labelled illustration only, not a second output of this page.

FAQs

How much EMI can I afford?
A common guide is 30 percent of monthly income, but total debts and living costs must also fit.

Is this wedding calculator exact?
No, it is an estimate for planning only, based on the numbers you enter.

Are these figures financial advice?
No, they are estimates for planning only, not financial advice. Check with a qualified adviser for decisions involving large sums.

Is a $1,500 EMI over 20 years good value for a wedding?
No wedding is worth 20 years of payments. Over 240 months, $1,500 a month is $360,000 of total EMI capacity, and a loan that runs that long will have cost far more in interest than the wedding day ever cost. The cap exposes the problem, it does not justify it.

Does a 20-year wedding loan affect a mortgage application?
Yes. Lenders count existing EMIs against income, so a $1,500 wedding EMI leaves less room for a house payment every month for as long as it runs. Clearing wedding debt before applying for a mortgage is usually worth more than a bigger wedding.

What if the couple wants to clear the loan early in year 8?
Early repayment is the best use of spare income: every month cleared early removes a $1,500 payment and all its future interest. Check for early-repayment charges first, then put bonuses and savings toward the loan rather than wedding extras.

Declining Balance Depreciation Calculator

Calculate first-year depreciation when a fixed percentage is applied to the shrinking balance.

—

Formula

First-year depreciation equals cost times the rate; later years apply the same rate to the remaining book value, floored at salvage value.

How it works

Declining balance front-loads depreciation, matching assets that lose most value early. The book value never drops below salvage value in this calculator.

FAQs

Why is early depreciation higher?
Because the rate applies to a larger balance in the first years.

When is this method used?
For vehicles, computers, and machinery that age fastest early on.

What is book value?
Cost minus depreciation taken so far.

Are these figures financial advice?
No — they are estimates for planning only, not financial advice. Check with a qualified adviser for decisions involving large sums.

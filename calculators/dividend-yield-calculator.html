Dividend Yield Calculator

Find dividend yield from annual dividend per share and share price.

—

Formula

Dividend yield equals annual dividend per share divided by share price, times 100.

How it works

Yield moves inversely with price: if the price falls, yield rises even if the dividend is unchanged. A very high yield can signal a price fall or a dividend at risk, not just generosity.

FAQs

Is a higher yield always better?
No — check whether the dividend is sustainable from earnings.

Does yield include price growth?
No, total return is yield plus price change.

Can dividends be cut?
Yes, companies can reduce or suspend dividends.

Are these figures financial advice?
No — they are estimates for planning only, not financial advice. Check with a qualified adviser for decisions involving large sums.

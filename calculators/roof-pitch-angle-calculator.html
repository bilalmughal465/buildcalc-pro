Roof Pitch Angle Calculator

Turn roof rise and run into a pitch angle in degrees and a pitch ratio.



—

Formula

Pitch angle = arctangent of (rise divided by run), converted from radians to degrees.

How it works

Enter the vertical rise and the horizontal run over the same section of roof, in any matching units. The angle helps with material choice, since very shallow pitches suit different coverings than steep ones. Always measure rise and run over the same roof section.

FAQs

What units can I use?
Any matching pair, such as metres and metres or feet and feet. Mixing units gives a wrong angle.

What is a steep pitch?
Above about 45 degrees is steep and harder to work on, while below about 15 degrees is shallow and needs careful waterproofing.

Does pitch decide the roofing material?
Partly. Each material has a minimum pitch, so check the manufacturer's guidance before choosing.

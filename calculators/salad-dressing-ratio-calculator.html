Salad Dressing Ratio Calculator

A classic vinaigrette is 3 parts oil to 1 part vinegar. Scale it by servings, using about 30 ml of oil per serving as a starting point.

—

Formula

Oil = servings x oil per serving. Vinegar = oil / 3.

How it works

The 3:1 ratio is a starting point, not a law. Sharper vinegar needs more oil; mellow vinegar needs less. Taste and adjust.

A teaspoon of mustard helps the dressing stay mixed instead of separating in seconds. Whisk the vinegar and mustard first, then drizzle in the oil.

FAQs

What oil works best?
A mild olive oil or neutral oil for everyday salads; a peppery extra-virgin oil when the dressing is the star.

How long does homemade dressing keep?
About a week in the fridge in a sealed jar. Shake well before using, since it separates.

Can I change the ratio?
Absolutely. 2:1 is tangier and lighter; 4:1 is mellow and rich. Adjust to your vinegar and taste.

Room Volume Calculator

Calculate the volume of a rectangular room from length, width and height.




—

Formula

Room volume = length multiplied by width multiplied by height.

How it works

Multiply the three room dimensions to get cubic volume. Volume matters for heating, cooling, ventilation and acoustics, since a tall room needs more air handling than a low room of the same floor area.

FAQs

What if the room is L-shaped?
Split it into rectangles, calculate each volume, and add them together.

Do I use internal dimensions?
Yes, measure inside wall to inside wall for usable room volume.

Why does volume matter?
Heating, cooling and fresh-air systems are sized on volume, not floor area alone.

Cycling Speed and Time Calculator

Convert between cycling distance, time and average speed in one step.

—

Formula

Average speed equals distance divided by time in hours.

How it works

Distance divided by time gives the average, including any slower sections. It says nothing about top speed or effort on hills.

FAQs

Why is my average lower than my cruising speed?
Stops, junctions and hills all drag the average down.

Does wind matter?
A lot. Headwinds cost more time than tailwinds save.

Can I use miles?
Convert miles to kilometres first (1 mile = 1.609 km).

Dog Water Intake Calculator

Estimate a dog's daily water range from body weight.

—

Formula

Daily water is roughly 40 to 60 ml per kilogram of body weight, with 50 ml per kilogram as a typical midpoint.

How it works

Weight is multiplied by the low and high ends of the range to give a daily band. Fresh water should always be available, and a sudden large change in drinking deserves attention.

FAQs

Does wet food change it?
Yes. Wet food supplies water, so drinking from a bowl drops.

Is more water always better?
No. Very high intake can signal a problem.

How do I measure it?
Fill a measured bowl and note how much is gone in 24 hours.

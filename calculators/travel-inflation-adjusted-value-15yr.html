Travel Inflation Adjusted Value Calculator (15 Years)

Estimate what an amount will be worth after inflation. This travel version uses a 15-year planning term as its starting point.

—

Formula

Future amount equals amount times one plus inflation rate to the power of years.

How it works

Inflation means the same amount buys less over time, so future prices are higher.

Worked example

Amount 10,000 dollars, inflation rate 3 percent, term 15 years. Using the page formula, the future amount is 10,000 dollars times one plus 3 percent, to the power of 15, which is 15,579.67 dollars. That is the calculator output for these defaults. The increase over today's amount is 15,579.67 dollars minus 10,000 dollars, which is 5,579.67 dollars. That is a rise of about 55.80 percent in total over 15 years, so a trip that costs 10,000 dollars today needs more than half as much again.

Who this term suits for a traveller: a traveller planning a long-delayed trip, such as a once-in-a-lifetime journey saved for over a working decade, where the fund must grow well past today's price or the trip will have to shrink.

FAQs

What does inflation do to money?
Inflation reduces what money can buy, so a sum today will need to be larger in future to buy the same things.

Is this travel calculator exact?
No, it is an estimate for planning only, based on the numbers you enter.

Are these figures financial advice?
No, they are estimates for planning only, not financial advice. Check with a qualified adviser for decisions involving large sums.

Why does the 15-year figure pass 15,000 dollars?
Because 10,000 dollars times 1.03 to the power of 15 is 15,579.67 dollars. The increase is 5,579.67 dollars, which is more than half the original sum again, because 15 years of compounding at 3 percent adds up to about 55.80 percent in total.

Does a 15-year travel fund need an inflation check each year?
Yes, because a single fixed rate will not match real prices for 15 years. If travel prices average 5 percent instead of 3 percent, the same trip would cost 20,789.28 dollars in 15 years, which is 5,209.61 dollars more than the 3 percent figure.

What happens if the trip is brought forward from 15 years to 10 years?
The cost falls to 13,439.16 dollars, saving 2,140.51 dollars compared with the 15-year figure, because 5 fewer years of compounding apply. Bringing a trip forward is one of the few reliable ways to beat inflation, since the traveller pays today's prices sooner.

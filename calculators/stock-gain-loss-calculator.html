Stock Gain or Loss Calculator

Calculate gain or loss from buying and selling shares, including fees.

—

Formula

Gain equals shares times sell price minus shares times buy price minus fees.

How it works

This is a pre-tax gain. Short-term and long-term tax treatment differ by country, so the after-tax result will be lower where capital gains tax applies.

FAQs

Does it include dividends?
No — add dividends received to the gain separately.

What if the result is negative?
That is a loss, which may offset gains elsewhere under local tax rules.

Why include fees?
Broker fees reduce the real gain, sometimes significantly on small trades.

Are these figures financial advice?
No — they are estimates for planning only, not financial advice. Check with a qualified adviser for decisions involving large sums.

Travel Simple Interest Total Calculator (15 Years)

Calculate simple interest and the total amount to repay. This travel version uses a 15-year planning term as its starting point.

—

The Interest figure is the total cost of borrowing over 15 years, and the Total figure is the principal plus that Interest, which is the full amount to repay by the end of the term.

Formula

Simple interest equals principal times rate times time, divided by 100.

How it works

Interest is charged only on the original principal, not on interest already added.

Worked example

Principal $10,000, annual rate 5 percent, time 15 years. Simple interest is charged only on the original principal, so each year adds 5 percent of $10,000, which is $500. Over 15 years the interest is 15 times $500, which is $7,500. The total amount to repay is the principal plus the interest, $10,000 plus $7,500, which is $17,500. That is the calculator's own result for these defaults, and it follows directly from the page formula: principal times rate times time, divided by 100.

Who this term suits for a traveller: a traveller funding a long-term base for travel, such as a holiday home or a campervan kept for many seasons, where the interest is large enough that the total cost deserves a second look before signing.

FAQs

What is simple interest?
Simple interest is interest charged only on the original amount, not on accumulated interest.

Is this travel calculator exact?
No, it is an estimate for planning only, based on the numbers you enter.

Are these figures financial advice?
No, they are estimates for planning only, not financial advice. Check with a qualified adviser for decisions involving large sums.

How does $7,500 interest build up over 15 years?
It builds up $500 at a time, one year after another, with no interest charged on interest. After 15 years that is 15 times $500, which is $7,500, and the total repaid is $17,500. The yearly figure never changes; only the number of years grows.

Should a traveller borrow for 15 years for a holiday home?
A holiday home used for 15 seasons can justify a longer loan, but the traveller should compare the $17,500 total with renting or saving first. Fifteen years of $500 interest is three quarters of the amount borrowed, so the home must be worth well more than its price to carry that cost.

Does simple interest beat compound interest over 15 years?
Yes, on the same $10,000 at 5 percent, compound interest over 15 years would cost about $10,789, because interest would be charged on interest each year. Simple interest costs $7,500, which is about $3,289 less, and the gap grows wider with every extra year.

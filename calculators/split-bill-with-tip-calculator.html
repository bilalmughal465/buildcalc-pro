Split Bill With Tip Calculator

Add the tip first, then divide the whole total — so the tip is shared fairly too.

—

Formula

Total = bill x (1 + tip % / 100). Each share = total / people.

How it works

Adding the tip before splitting means everyone contributes to it in proportion — simpler than tipping separately afterwards.

If one person pays the whole bill, this also tells them exactly how much to collect from each friend.

FAQs

Should the tip be split evenly too?
Yes, when everyone shared the meal. It keeps the math simple and the split fair.

What if people want different tip percentages?
Agree on one percentage first. Splitting with two different tips gets messy fast.

Do I tip before or after tax?
Most people tip on the pre-tax amount, but tipping on the total is also common and simpler.

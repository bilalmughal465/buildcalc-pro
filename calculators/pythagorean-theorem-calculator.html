Pythagorean Theorem Calculator

Find the hypotenuse of a right triangle from its two shorter sides.

—

Formula

For a right triangle, a squared + b squared = c squared, where c is the hypotenuse.

How it works

Enter the two shorter sides. The calculator squares each, adds them, and takes the square root. Sides 3 and 4 give hypotenuse 5.

Farm Credit Card Monthly Interest Calculator (25 Years)

Estimate monthly interest on a card balance. This farm version uses a 25-year planning term as its starting point.

—

Formula

Monthly interest equals balance times annual rate divided by 100 divided by 12.

How it works

Paying only interest leaves the balance unchanged. Paying more than interest reduces the balance.

Worked example

Balance $5,000, annual rate 18%, term 25 years. Using the page formula, monthly interest is $5,000 times 18 divided by 100 divided by 12, which is $75.00 a month. That is the calculator's own result for these defaults, and it is the same on every page in this group because the term in years is not an input on this page.

As an illustration only, not the output of this page, if the balance stayed at $5,000 and only the interest was paid every month, the interest paid over 25 years would be $75.00 times 300 months, which is $22,500.00, and the $5,000 balance would still be owed at the end. That illustration assumes no new spending, no fees, no rate change, and no payment above the interest, so in real life the total would differ if the balance moved.

Twenty-five years of $75.00 a month is $22,500.00, four and a half times the original balance, paid without reducing the debt by a single dollar. That is a working generation of farm payments spent on interest alone, enough to have bought serious farm equipment outright, yet the household would still owe the original $5,000.

Who this term suits for a farm household: a farm household that needs a shock figure, not a borrowing plan: for example, a family reviewing old debts before passing the farm on, and discovering that a card balance has quietly drained harvest income for decades. The 25-year page exists to make the lifetime cost visible, so the balance is cleared or refinanced rather than handed on to the next generation.

FAQs

Why is card interest high?
Card rates are usually higher than loan rates, so balances grow quickly if only minimum payments are made.

Is this farm calculator exact?
No, it is an estimate for planning only, based on the numbers you enter.

Are these figures financial advice?
No, they are estimates for planning only, not financial advice. Check with a qualified adviser for decisions involving large sums.

How does 25 years of card interest reach $22,500.00?
Because $75.00 a month times 300 months is $22,500.00, and interest-only payments keep the balance at $5,000, so the charge never falls. Each year adds $900.00 of interest, and after 25 years those years add up to four and a half times the amount borrowed.

Could a farm family really pay $22,500.00 and still owe $5,000?
On this illustration, yes, because paying only interest never touches the balance. That is the trap of card debt carried for decades: the payments feel manageable at $75.00 a month, but the debt itself never shrinks, so the household pays again and again for the same $5,000.

What does a 25-year card balance mean for farm succession?
It means the next generation could inherit a debt that has already cost $22,500.00 in interest and still stands at $5,000. Before any handover, the balance should be cleared or moved to a cheaper, repaying loan, so the farm is not passed on with a card debt still charging $75.00 a month.

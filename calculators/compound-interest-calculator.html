Compound Interest Calculator

See how an investment grows when interest is earned on both the principal and past interest.

—

Formula

Future value equals principal times one plus the rate divided by compounds per year, raised to compounds times years.

How it works

Compounding frequency matters: monthly compounding grows slightly faster than yearly compounding at the same rate. Enter your starting amount and let the calculator show the final balance and the interest earned.

FAQs

What does compounds per year mean?
How often interest is added — 12 for monthly, 4 for quarterly, 1 for yearly.

Why does it grow faster than simple interest?
Because each period's interest starts earning interest of its own.

Is tax included?
No, this is a before-tax estimate.

Are these figures financial advice?
No — they are estimates for planning only, not financial advice. Check with a qualified adviser for decisions involving large sums.

Estimates only, not financial advice. Figures are approximate and do not include all fees, taxes, or changing rates unless stated.

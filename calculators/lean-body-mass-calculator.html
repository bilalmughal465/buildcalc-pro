Lean Body Mass Calculator

Estimate lean body mass from total weight and body fat percentage.

—

Formula

Lean body mass equals total weight multiplied by one minus body fat percentage.

How it works

Fat mass is weight times body-fat fraction, and everything else — muscle, bone, water and organs — is the remainder. The result is only as good as the body-fat figure you enter.

FAQs

Is lean mass the same as muscle?
No. Muscle is part of lean mass, along with bone, water and organs.

Why track it?
It shows whether weight change is mostly fat or mostly lean tissue.

How accurate is it?
It inherits the error of the body-fat estimate.

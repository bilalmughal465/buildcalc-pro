Backfill Volume Calculator

Work out backfill needed by subtracting the buried structure volume from excavation volume.



—

Formula

Backfill volume = excavation volume minus volume of the structure buried in it, such as footings, pipes or tanks.

How it works

Take the hole's volume and subtract everything that stays buried inside it. What remains is the space to fill back. Add a compaction allowance when ordering, since loose fill settles and compacts below its loose volume.

FAQs

Should I add compaction allowance?
Yes, typically ten to twenty percent extra, depending on material and compaction method.

What if the structure is larger than the hole?
That cannot happen in practice, so check the measurements if the result is zero.

Can excavated soil be reused?
Often yes, if it is suitable and free of debris, but unsuitable soil must be replaced with proper fill.

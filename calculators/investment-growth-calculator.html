Investment Growth Calculator

Project how an investment grows with a starting amount, regular contributions, and return.

Formula

Growth combines compounding on the starting amount with the future value of a monthly contribution stream.

How it works

This shows the power of steady contributions plus compounding. It assumes the same return every year and no withdrawals, taxes, or fees.

FAQs

Is the return guaranteed?
No — market returns vary year to year.

Are fees included?
No, subtract fund fees from the return for a net view.

What if I stop contributing?
The starting amount keeps growing, but the final balance will be lower.

Are these figures financial advice?
No — they are estimates for planning only, not financial advice. Check with a qualified adviser for decisions involving large sums.

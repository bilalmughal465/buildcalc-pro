Rent vs Buy Comparison Calculator

Roughly compare monthly renting with monthly owning, side by side.

—

A positive difference means monthly owning costs more than monthly renting in this comparison, while a negative difference would mean owning costs less per month.

Formula

Monthly owning cost is the mortgage payment on price minus down payment plus monthly tax, insurance, and HOA; compare it with rent.

How it works

This is a monthly cash comparison only — it ignores home price growth, rent rises, maintenance, selling costs, and tax effects, so it is a starting point, not a buy-or-rent verdict.

Worked example

For example, monthly rent of $1,500 compared with a $300,000 home, a $60,000 down payment, a 6.5% mortgage over 30 years, and $500 a month for tax, insurance, and HOA gives monthly owning of $2,016.96, which is $516.96 more than renting.

FAQs

Why is owning often higher at first?
Mortgage plus ownership costs can exceed rent, especially with a small down payment.

What costs are missing?
Maintenance, repairs, and selling costs on the owning side.

Does it say which is better long term?
No — long-term value depends on price growth and how long you stay.

Are these figures financial advice?
No — they are estimates for planning only, not financial advice. Check with a qualified adviser for decisions involving large sums.

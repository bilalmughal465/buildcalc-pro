Chore Time Split Calculator

Divide the week's total chore time by the number of people to see each person's fair share.

—

Formula

Each share = total chore time / number of people.

How it works

Time is the fairest way to split chores, because tasks differ in effort: thirty minutes of scrubbing is not the same as thirty minutes of tidying, but time keeps it simple.

Rotating tasks weekly stops one person always getting the worst job — fairness over time matters more than a perfect single week.

FAQs

Is time really the fairest split?
It is the simplest fair measure. Splitting by task count alone ignores that some chores take much longer.

What if someone works longer hours?
Households often adjust shares then — the split can be weighted, but it should be agreed openly.

How do I estimate total chore time?
Time each regular chore for a week, then add them up. Most households are surprised by the total.

Gift Budget Split Calculator

Divide your total gift budget by the number of recipients to see a fair amount per person.

—

Formula

Per recipient = total budget / number of recipients.

How it works

An even split is a starting point. If one recipient is a close family member and others are acquaintances, weighting the split is perfectly fair.

Setting the total first stops the common trap of buying each gift separately and discovering the total far too late.

FAQs

Should every gift cost the same?
Not necessarily. An even split is fair as a default, but closeness and occasion can justify different amounts.

What about wrapping and cards?
Include them in the budget if money is tight — they add up across several gifts.

How do I handle a group gift?
Divide the group gift's cost among contributors first, then treat each person's share as part of their own budget.

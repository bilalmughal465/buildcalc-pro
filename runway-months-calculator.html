Runway Months Calculator

Estimate how many months current cash will last at the present monthly burn rate.



—

Formula

Runway in months = cash available divided by monthly burn rate.

How it works

Divide the cash in the bank by what the business spends net each month. The answer tells you roughly when money runs out if nothing changes. Most founders aim to raise money or reach profit well before runway hits zero.

FAQs

What if burn changes?
Runway changes with it. Recalculate whenever spending or income shifts, and use a conservative burn figure for planning.

Does runway include future income?
This simple version does not. If income is coming in, use net burn, which already accounts for it.

How much runway is safe?
There is no single rule, but planning with only a month or two left leaves little room for delays.

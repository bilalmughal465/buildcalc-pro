Everyday Time Zone Converter

Convert a UTC hour to another time zone by offset.

The result shown above is the hour in the other time zone, given as Local hour with the hour on a 24-hour clock.

Worked example

For example, with a UTC hour of 12 and an offset of 5, 12 + 5 = 17, so the result is Local hour: 17:00.

Frequently asked questions

Does this handle daylight saving?

No. This is a simple offset converter. Daylight saving can change the offset by one hour.

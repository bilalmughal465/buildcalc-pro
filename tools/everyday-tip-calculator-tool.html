The result shown above gives the tip amount for the bill and tip percentage you entered, plus the total bill including that tip.

Worked example

For example, with a bill of $50 and a tip of 15 percent, the tip is 50 x 15 / 100 = $7.50, and the total is $50 + $7.50 = $57.50.

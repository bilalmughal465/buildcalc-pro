Hours Between Times Calculator

Get the elapsed time between two clock times as a decimal number of hours.

—

Formula

Decimal hours = total minutes between the two times divided by 60. Overnight spans wrap past midnight.

How it works

Pick the two times. The calculator reports elapsed time both in minutes and as decimal hours, the form payroll and billing systems usually need — 7 hours 30 minutes becomes 7.50 hours.

FAQs

Why decimal hours instead of hours and minutes?
Payroll and invoicing usually multiply a rate by decimal hours, so 7.50 hours is easier to bill than 7 hours 30 minutes.

Is 7 hours 30 minutes really 7.50 hours?
Yes. 30 minutes is half an hour, so 7 + 0.50 = 7.50 hours.

What about overnight shifts?
An end time earlier than the start is read as the next day.

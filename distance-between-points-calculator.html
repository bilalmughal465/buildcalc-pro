Distance Between Points Calculator

Find the straight-line distance between two points on a flat plane.

—

Formula

Distance = square root of ((x2 − x1) squared + (y2 − y1) squared).

How it works

Enter two points. The calculator applies the distance formula, which comes from the Pythagorean theorem. Points (0,0) and (3,4) are 5 units apart.

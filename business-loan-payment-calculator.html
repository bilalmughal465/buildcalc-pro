FAQs

Are business rates higher?
Often yes, because business lending carries different risks and terms.

What about fees?
Origination and other fees raise the true cost above this payment.

Should I compare total interest?
Yes — compare total paid, not just the monthly figure.

Are these figures financial advice?
No — they are estimates for planning only, not financial advice. Check with a qualified adviser for decisions involving large sums.

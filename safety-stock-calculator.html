Safety Stock Calculator

Estimate extra stock to hold for demand spikes during supplier lead time.




—

Formula

Safety stock = (maximum daily usage minus average daily usage) multiplied by lead time in days.

How it works

Subtract typical daily sales from the busiest realistic daily sales, then multiply by how many days a delivery takes. The buffer absorbs busy days while waiting for stock, without holding far more than needed.

FAQs

How do I know maximum daily usage?
Look at recent sales records and pick a realistic busy day, not a one-off record spike.

Does this cover late deliveries?
Only partly. If suppliers are often late, use a longer lead time in the calculation as well.

Is more safety stock always safer?
It prevents stockouts but ties up cash and space, so balance the cost of a stockout against holding cost.

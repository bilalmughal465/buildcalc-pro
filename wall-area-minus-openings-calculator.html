Wall Area Minus Openings Calculator

Find net paintable or coverable wall area by subtracting doors and windows from gross wall area.




—

Formula

Gross wall area = length multiplied by height. Net area = gross area minus total area of doors, windows and other openings.

How it works

Measure the wall's length and height, then subtract the combined area of every door, window and opening in it. The net figure is what paint, wallpaper or cladding actually needs to cover, so it avoids over-ordering.

FAQs

How do I total the openings?
Measure each door and window, multiply width by height for each, and add them all together.

Should I subtract skirting or trim?
No, those are small compared with the wall and are usually ignored for coverage estimates.

What about several walls?
Calculate each wall separately and add the net areas for the room total.

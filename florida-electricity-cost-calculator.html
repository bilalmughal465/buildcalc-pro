Florida electricity rates and bills (2024)

The average Florida household paid 14.14 cents per kWh, which is 14% below the U.S. average of 16.48. It ranked number 33 out of 51 (50 states plus DC) from highest price. Customers used 1,104 kWh a month and paid $156.09, against 863 kWh and $142.26 nationally. In Florida, air conditioning runs for much of the year. Source: EIA Table 5A, 2024 Average Monthly Bill, Residential.

Florida customers are mostly served by investor-owned utilities, municipal utilities and co-ops, and there is no retail electricity choice. Bills are driven by air conditioning for much of the year, so summer bills run well above the annual average.

How to calculate

Formula: monthly kWh = watts ÷ 1,000 × hours per day × days per month. Cost = monthly kWh × rate.

Worked example: 1,000 watts, 5 hours per day, 30 days, at the Florida average of $0.1414 per kWh. Monthly use = 150 kWh, monthly cost = $21.21. At the U.S. average the same use costs $24.72.

Frequently asked questions

How much does electricity cost per kWh in Florida?

The 2024 EIA average residential price in Florida was 14.14 cents per kWh. Your own rate depends on your utility and plan.

What is the average electric bill in Florida?

$156.09 per month in 2024, based on 1,104 kWh of average monthly use.

Is electricity in Florida cheaper than the national average?

Yes, the price is 14% below the U.S. average of 16.48 cents per kWh. The bill is above the national average of $142.26 because Florida households use more electricity.

Why is my bill different from this estimate?

These are statewide averages. Delivery charges, fixed fees, plan type, season and your own appliances change the real number, so check your bill.

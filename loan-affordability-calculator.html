Loan Affordability Calculator

Estimate the maximum loan you can afford from a monthly payment, rate, and term.

—

Formula

Maximum loan equals payment times one minus one plus monthly rate to the power of negative payments, divided by the monthly rate.

How it works

This reverses the payment formula. It shows the loan size, not the home price — add your down payment for the price, and remember taxes and insurance on top.

FAQs

Why work backwards?
Most budgets start from a monthly payment, not a loan size.

Does it include tax and insurance?
No — reserve part of your monthly budget for those.

What if rates fall?
The same payment affords a larger loan.

Are these figures financial advice?
No — they are estimates for planning only, not financial advice. Check with a qualified adviser for decisions involving large sums.

Estimates only, not financial advice. Figures are approximate and do not include all fees, taxes, or changing rates unless stated.

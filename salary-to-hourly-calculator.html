Salary to Hourly Calculator

Convert an annual salary into an hourly rate based on hours worked.

—

Formula

Hourly rate equals annual salary divided by hours per week times weeks per year.

How it works

Paid holidays and overtime change the picture, so adjust weeks or hours to match your real schedule. This gives a gross rate before tax.

FAQs

Why 52 weeks?
That is a full working year; use fewer weeks if you take unpaid leave.

Does it include benefits?
No, benefits are extra value on top of the cash rate.

Can I compare job offers this way?
Yes — hourly rate plus benefits gives a fairer comparison than salary alone.

Are these figures financial advice?
No — they are estimates for planning only, not financial advice. Check with a qualified adviser for decisions involving large sums.

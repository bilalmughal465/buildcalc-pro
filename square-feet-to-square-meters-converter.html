Convert square feet to square meters instantly with a simple, free calculator.

Formula

Square meters = Square feet × 0.092903. Multiply the value in square feet by 0.092903 to get the value in square meters.

How it works

Enter a value in square feet. The calculator multiplies it by the conversion factor and shows the equivalent in square meters, rounded to at most four decimal places so long decimals stay readable. Negative values are converted the same way, since the factor applies in both directions of the scale.

One square feet equals 0.092903 square meters. Multiply any value in square feet by 0.092903 to get square meters.

Is this square feet to square meters conversion exact?
One square foot is defined as exactly 0.09290304 square meters, so the conversion is exact before rounding.

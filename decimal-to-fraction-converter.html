Decimal to Fraction Calculator

Convert a decimal into a fraction in its simplest form.

—

Formula

Write the decimal as a fraction over a power of ten, then divide the top and bottom by their greatest common divisor to simplify it.

How it works

Type a decimal such as 0.75. The calculator writes it over 100, then simplifies by the greatest common divisor, giving 3/4.

What should I enter?
Enter ordinary numbers in the boxes above. If a value does not make sense for this calculation, the page will ask you to check it instead of showing a wrong answer.

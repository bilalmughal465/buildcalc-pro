Proportion Calculator

Solve a proportion of the form a over b equals x over d, finding the missing value x.

—

Formula

From a ÷ b = x ÷ d, cross-multiplying gives x = (a × d) ÷ b.

How it works

Enter a, b, and d. The calculator cross-multiplies and divides to find the missing value that keeps both fractions equal.

FAQs

What does the Proportion do?
It takes the values you enter and works out the proportion for you, showing the result straight away.

What should I enter?
Enter ordinary numbers in the boxes above. If a value does not make sense for this calculation, the page will ask you to check it instead of showing a wrong answer.

Can I use the result for homework or everyday maths?
Yes. It is a general-purpose maths helper; for exam or professional work, still check the steps against your own working.

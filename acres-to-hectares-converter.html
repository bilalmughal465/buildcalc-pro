Acres to Hectares Converter

Convert acres to hectares instantly with a simple, free calculator.

—

Formula

Hectares = Acres × 0.404686. Multiply the value in acres by 0.404686 to get the value in hectares.

How it works

Enter a value in acres. The calculator multiplies it by the conversion factor and shows the equivalent in hectares, rounded to at most four decimal places so long decimals stay readable. Negative values are converted the same way, since the factor applies in both directions of the scale.

FAQs

How many hectares are in 1 acres?
One acres equals 0.404686 hectares. Multiply any value in acres by 0.404686 to get hectares.

Is this acres to hectares conversion exact?
One acre equals 4,046.8564224 square meters and one hectare equals 10,000 square meters, giving this exact factor before rounding.

When would I need a acres to hectares conversion?
Any time a measurement, label, or instruction is given in acres and you need to work, compare, or record it in hectares — for example while shopping, travelling, cooking, studying, or planning a project.

How to calculate sand

Sand is sold by volume or by weight. For a rectangular area, multiply length by width by depth, with everything in feet, then convert to cubic yards by dividing by 27. To get tons, multiply cubic yards by 1.3.

Formula: cubic yards = length (ft) × width (ft) × depth (in ÷ 12) × (1 + waste %) ÷ 27, and tons = cubic yards × 1.3

Worked example: 20 ft × 10 ft × 4 in area

Volume = 20 × 10 × (4 ÷ 12) = 66.7 ft³. Add 10% waste: 73.3 ft³. Divide by 27: 2.72 cubic yards, which is 3.5 tons.

Which sand?

Use washed concrete sand for paver beds and mixes. Play sand is finer and packs too tight for construction work.

Frequently asked questions

How much sand do I need for a 20x10 area?

A 20 ft by 10 ft area at 4 inches deep needs about 2.72 cubic yards with 10% waste, which is roughly 3.5 tons.

How many tons are in a cubic yard of sand?

About 1.3 tons, though it varies with moisture. This calculator uses 1.3.

How deep should sand be under pavers?

About 1 inch of bedding sand over 4 to 6 inches of compacted gravel base.

What type of sand should I use?

Washed concrete sand for paver beds and mixes. Play sand is finer and not for construction.

Are the prices accurate?

Prices are editable estimates. The defaults are typical US figures, and your local price can be higher or lower. Delivery fees are not included.

Income Tax Estimate Calculator

Estimate tax from income and a rate you enter — no rate is assumed for you.

—

Formula

Taxable income equals income minus deductions, and tax equals taxable income times the rate divided by 100.

How it works

Real tax systems use brackets, credits, and local rules, so this single-rate estimate is a rough planning figure only. Enter the effective rate you expect, or a marginal rate for an extra dollar of income.

FAQs

Why no rate built in?
Rates differ by country, region, and income, so entering your own rate avoids a wrong assumption.

What is an effective rate?
Total tax divided by total income — usually lower than the top marginal rate.

Is this a filing figure?
No — use official tables or a tax professional for filing.

Are these figures financial advice?
No — they are estimates for planning only, not financial advice. Check with a qualified adviser for decisions involving large sums.

Savings Goal Calculator

Find the monthly deposit needed to reach a savings target by a chosen date.

—

Formula

The required monthly deposit comes from the future-value-of-an-annuity formula, adjusted for the growth of your current savings.

How it works

Enter what you want, what you already have, and how long you have. The calculator assumes deposits at the end of each month and a steady rate.

FAQs

What if I already have more than the target?
Then no monthly deposit is needed — you are already there.

Does the rate matter much over short periods?
Less over months, more over years.

What if I miss a month?
You would need slightly larger later deposits to catch up.

Are these figures financial advice?
No — they are estimates for planning only, not financial advice. Check with a qualified adviser for decisions involving large sums.

Estimates only, not financial advice. Figures are approximate and do not include all fees, taxes, or changing rates unless stated.

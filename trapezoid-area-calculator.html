Trapezoid Area Calculator

Find the area of a trapezoid from its two parallel sides and its height.

—

Formula

Trapezoid area = ½ × (parallel side a + parallel side b) × height.

How it works

Enter the two parallel sides and the distance between them. The calculator averages the parallel sides and multiplies by the height.

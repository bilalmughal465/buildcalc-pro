Subtract Days from Date Calculator

Subtract a number of days from a date and get the resulting calendar date.

—

Formula

Result date = start date minus the number of days, with month and year rollover handled by the calendar.

How it works

Pick a start date and enter how many days to subtract. The calculator steps back across month and year boundaries, which is handy for finding deadlines counted backwards from an event.

FAQs

Is this the same as adding negative days?
Yes. Subtracting 30 days gives the same date as adding −30 days.

Does it skip weekends?
No. It subtracts calendar days, weekends included.

Can the result go into a previous year?
Yes. Subtracting enough days rolls back across year boundaries.
